# Editorial review — Flight terms

- Source: `content/encyclopedia/terminology/flight.md`
- Reviewed: 2026-11-02
- Scope: Inspected glossary terms and reference structure, without rendering the site or independent SME validation
- Scope: 49 glossary entries

## 1. Is it too long?

Moderate-sized mixed fixed-/rotor-wing glossary. It should remain lookup-oriented, with terse but complete definitions and no repeated chapter-length teaching paragraphs.

## 2. Imagery and interactivity

Prioritise anchors, cross-links and distinctions (e.g. ETL versus translational lift; stall versus vortex ring state). Existing fixed- and rotor-wing module illustrations belong there.

## 3. Learning outcomes

Explain central aerodynamics and control vocabulary in context and distinguish terms that sound similar but describe different phenomena.

For a terminology page the outcome is rapid, correct recognition and disambiguation, not completion of a narrative lesson.

## 4. Missing important information

Check inconsistent 'corner speed' versus 'corner airspeed' naming, fixed-wing versus helicopter meanings, and model-specific AOA 'units' qualifiers.

## 5. Simulator relevance and differences

DCS/BMS aircraft differ in simulated aerodynamics, failure effects and cockpit indicators; keep physics definitions primary and simulator examples clearly bounded.

Explain applicability to specific simulator/module/version only where verified. Classify implemented, simplified, unavailable or unverified, and keep documented real-world terminology distinct from simulation features. Use existing sim-panel styling where applicable.

## Recommended action

Improve term discoverability, usage context and cross-links before trimming authoritative definitions. SME source review remains required for doctrinal changes.
