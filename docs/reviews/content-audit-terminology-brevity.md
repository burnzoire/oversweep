# Editorial review — Brevity words

- Source: `content/encyclopedia/terminology/brevity.md`
- Reviewed: 2026-11-02
- Scope: Inspected glossary terms and reference structure, without rendering the site or independent SME validation
- Scope: 60 glossary entries

## 1. Is it too long?

Length arises from 60 definitions, not prose. Don't shorten doctrinal definitions simply to hit a word target. The introductory explanation should be concise and searchable.

## 2. Imagery and interactivity

Already includes a contextual AWACS photo. A term list needs a fast alphabetical/filter interface, not decorative images or interactive radio minigames.

## 3. Learning outcomes

Identify standard military brevity terms and choose correctly between related words (e.g. BANDIT vs BOGEY; TALLY vs VISUAL; BINGO vs WINCHESTER).

For a terminology page the outcome is rapid, correct recognition and disambiguation, not completion of a narrative lesson.

## 4. Missing important information

Make contexts and ambiguous cross-service meanings prominent. Verify edition and exact definitions; changes to doctrine must be sourced. Link directly to the radio procedures module.

## 5. Simulator relevance and differences

DCS/BMS AI ATC doesn't necessarily recognise doctrinal brevity; human multiplayer comms and scripted calls are distinct. Explicitly classify official terminology versus simulator-supported communications.

Explain applicability to specific simulator/module/version only where verified. Classify implemented, simplified, unavailable or unverified, and keep documented real-world terminology distinct from simulation features. Use existing sim-panel styling where applicable.

## Recommended action

Improve term discoverability, usage context and cross-links before trimming authoritative definitions. SME source review remains required for doctrinal changes.
