# Editorial audit — GAU-8/A Avenger

- Content: `content/encyclopedia/ordnance/gau-8.md`
- Reviewed: 2026-11-02
- Review type: Editorial and learning design; **not** independent aviation SME validation
- Recommendation: **Keep and refine** — this should remain a flagship featured article.

## 1. Is it too long?

**Mostly appropriate depth, but repetitive.** The page has a substantial structured specification plate followed by prose that repeats the weight, length, drum capacity, rate of fire, and A-10 installation. Keep the authoritative plate and reduce the narrative repetition. Retain the seven-barrel mechanism, cartridge-role distinctions, dispersion calculation and installation-specific caveats: these make the page distinctive. Avoid shortening it into a generic weapon summary.

Suggested edit: open with a two-paragraph account of *why the gun is distinctive*, then mechanism, ammunition, performance/dispersion, and integration. Give the reader a direct path to the main facts rather than another specification inventory.

## 2. Imagery and interactivity

**Imagery: worthwhile improvement.** Current photo shows the detached gun. Add a legally reusable cutaway or annotated installation image showing muzzle, barrel group, drum, feed and their position within the A-10, if available with verified rights. A small static schematic of the rotary firing cycle would communicate the mechanism more efficiently than several paragraphs. Do **not** add a game-like firing simulator merely for engagement.

The existing 5 m dispersion conversion would suit a restrained, **static** angle-to-distance diagram, clearly labelled as geometrical illustration rather than a combat grouping prediction.

## 3. Intended learning outcomes

After reading, the reader should be able to:
1. Distinguish the GAU-8 gun from the complete A-10 ammunition/feed installation.
2. Explain why seven externally driven barrels distribute firing load.
3. Distinguish HEI, API and TP ammunition by role without inferring a universal ammunition mix.
4. Interpret rate-of-fire and dispersion figures with their source and installation limits.
5. Identify how mass, recoil and ammunition handling affect aircraft integration.

**Assessment:** all five are substantially supported; the installation distinction is particularly strong.

## 4. Missing important content

- An installation view, not another general specification.
- Consider a short, sourced explanation of what makes the A-10 installation unusual mechanically, without repeating the A-10 article.
- Label the cited 3,900 versus 4,200 rounds/minute numbers directly in the specification/overview as *installation reference* versus *manufacturer gun maximum*; currently this distinction appears later.
- Primary-source coverage has improved (General Dynamics), but most frontmatter facts still point to secondary source [1]. A separate source audit should assign primary citations where available.
