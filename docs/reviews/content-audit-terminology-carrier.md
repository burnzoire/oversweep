## 1. Is it too long?

Seventy-three entries are reference rather than a long essay. Duplicate labels such as Bingo, Bullseye and Charlie appear in the file: ensure disambiguation/usage is explicit, not accidental repetition.

## 2. Imagery and interactivity

An alphabetical glossary with contextual anchors is sufficient. Existing Case III and ship operations modules provide diagrams; link instead of replicating.

## 3. Learning outcomes

Interpret marshal, BRC/final bearing, ball, bolter, waveoff and shipboard deck/aircraft terminology with context.

For a terminology page the outcome is rapid, correct recognition and disambiguation, not completion of a narrative lesson.

## 4. Missing important information

Check duplicates for distinct senses, edition scope and ship/aircraft specificity. Keep CV, amphibious and rotary-wing usages distinct.

## 5. Simulator relevance and differences

DCS carrier ATC, deck crew and LSO callouts support a subset of naval vocabulary; mark real-world-only terms without inventing in-game support.

Explain applicability to specific simulator/module/version only where verified. Classify implemented, simplified, unavailable or unverified, and keep documented real-world terminology distinct from simulation features. Use existing sim-panel styling where applicable.

## Recommended action

Improve term discoverability, usage context and cross-links before trimming authoritative definitions. SME source review remains required for doctrinal changes.
