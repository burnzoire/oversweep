## Unverified or unresolved

- MAIN PWR lever-lock: recorded as latched; no release gesture is simulated (Nathan, 2026-11-16).
- ENG FEED detents: NORM 44°, FWD 52° either side of AFT as printed in the image.
- FUEL MASTER bushing hidden by the guard (medium toggle assumed); TANK INERTING lever points at the camera.
- ELEC plate left, top and bottom edges are cropped in the reference.
- Lettering is a condensed sans stretched to each legend's measured width, not MS 33558 glyphs.
- Physical scale: DZUS heads fixed at 8.5 mm from the FUEL panel taken as 5.75 in wide.
