# Editorial review — Air combat terms

- Source: `content/encyclopedia/terminology/air-combat.md`
- Reviewed: 2026-11-02
- Scope: Inspected glossary terms and reference structure, without rendering the site or independent SME validation
- Scope: 21 glossary entries

## 1. Is it too long?

Relatively short. Definitions like Bubble and Control zone need careful scope, but should not be collapsed into one-sentence approximations that omit the T-45 training context.

## 2. Imagery and interactivity

No need for diagrams inside the glossary: direct links to the existing BFM geometry, pursuit and overshoot interactive figures would teach more.

## 3. Learning outcomes

Distinguish pursuit, 3/9-line/aspect, one-/two-circle flow, energy-related terms and training deconfliction terminology.

For a terminology page the outcome is rapid, correct recognition and disambiguation, not completion of a narrative lesson.

## 4. Missing important information

Several numeric definitions come from T-45-specific training. Flag that they are not universal aircraft limits and ensure consistent links to BFM sections.

## 5. Simulator relevance and differences

In DCS/BMS BFM physics and AI depend on flight/AI models; terms are real-world conceptual tools, not proof that sim manoeuvres have identical limits.

Explain applicability to specific simulator/module/version only where verified. Classify implemented, simplified, unavailable or unverified, and keep documented real-world terminology distinct from simulation features. Use existing sim-panel styling where applicable.

## Recommended action

Improve term discoverability, usage context and cross-links before trimming authoritative definitions. SME source review remains required for doctrinal changes.
