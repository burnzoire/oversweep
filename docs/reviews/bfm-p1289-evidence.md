## Models

- **Level turn (Figure 3).** Standard flight mechanics, not from P-1289: bank
  φ = arccos(1/n), r = V²/(g√(n² − 1)), ω = g√(n² − 1)/V, V true airspeed,
  g = 9.80665 m/s². Treats KIAS as TAS. Reference cases in
  `tests/lessons/bfm-lesson.test.mjs` (2 G at 300 kt: 60°, 4,601 ft, 6.31°/s;
  7.33 G at 410 kt: 82.2°, 2,050 ft, 19.3°/s).
- **Radial G (Figure 4).** RG = n − cos θ, θ pitch around the loop. Reproduces
  the p. 4-5 example (4 G: 3 at the bottom, 5 at the top).
- **Pursuit (Figure 2).** Kinematic illustration only: constant 350 kt for both,
  bandit on a 3,000 ft circle, fighter turn rate capped at 15°/s, aim point 1.5 s
  of bandit flight ahead, on or behind the bandit. Shows only the qualitative
  range behaviour described on p. 5-4.
