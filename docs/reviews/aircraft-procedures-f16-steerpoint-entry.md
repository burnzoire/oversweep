# Aircraft Procedures — F-16C steerpoint entry & navigation (issue #398)

## Scope
A guided exercise on the shared cockpit panels (ICP, DED, HUD, EHSI): from a navigation-ready jet with steerpoint 4 selected, open the STPT page, select the empty steerpoint 5, type its latitude, longitude and elevation, return to the CNI page, then check the new steerpoint on the HUD (number, distance, time to go, diamond) and the EHSI (NAV bearing and range, course set). The last step is the completion check that steerpoint 5 is the active one on all three displays.

## Sources
- Eagle Dynamics, DCS F-16C Viper Early Access Guide (EN, 16 August 2026): steerpoints and the STPT page pp. 205–208 (STPT figure p. 207), editing a steerpoint pp. 212–213 (the DEST DIR entry figures, including the empty steerpoint), CNI page p. 103, navigation by steerpoints, the HUD and the EHSI pp. 228–232. No community guide is cited or followed.
- The position typed (N 36°35.822′, E 036°35.014′, 892 ft) is the guide's STPT figure's data, given to steerpoint 5.

## Illustrative, not sourced
- Aircraft position, heading (197°), ground speed (391 kt), steerpoint 4 (19 NM on 195°) and steerpoint 5 (31.4 NM on 185°, time to go 4:49). The HUD diamond and steering cue offsets are derived from those bearings; the cue's pinned position at 10° is a guess at its lateral boundary.
- Steerpoint 4's position (N 35°44.295′, E 037°02.898′, 823 ft) is the guide's DEST DIR figure for steerpoint 4. Steerpoint 5 is shown empty (the guide's empty-steerpoint figure) when selected; whether an unused steerpoint is empty in a given DCS mission depends on the mission's route.
- The course knob turns 5° a click on this page; deviation scale as in the TACAN procedure.

## Unverified in the sim
- DED states part-way through typing (characters left to right, the ° . ′ marks as reached, elevation as digits before FT) are not in the guide, which shows only the typed and accepted fields. Whether the asterisks stay on a field after ENTR follows the guide's step order (DCS down moves them on).
- That the STPT page asterisks start on the steerpoint number follows the guide's STPT figure and the DEST DIR steps.
