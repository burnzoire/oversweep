## 1. Is it too long?

The design origin and Battle of Britain history precede the Mk IX's own raison d'être. Compress origins and foreground the Mk IX's response to the Fw 190, Merlin changes and operational trade-offs.

## 2. Does it need imagery or interaction?

Two photos exist. A brief Mk V versus Mk IX comparison or labelled wing/engine changes would be preferable to another portrait.

Inspect currently rendered photos and figures before adding any visual. A high-quality explanatory static diagram is preferred to unnecessary interaction.

## 3. Learning outcomes

Identify Mk IX's purpose, how engine and supercharger choices affected altitude performance, and which features vary between Spitfire marks.

These are the useful reader takeaways and should determine what prose to retain.

## 4. Important omissions

Battle of Britain context must not imply Mk IX participation; distinguish mark-specific speed and weapon claims, and source extreme dive-speed anecdotes.

## 5. Simulator relevance and differences

DCS Spitfire LF Mk IX is a specific configuration, not all Mk IX variants; check boost/fuel, engine management and flight-model interpretation.

Use a distinct sim-specific panel as appropriate. Verify each simulation claim against an explicit simulator, module, version and documentation or observation. Classify as **modelled**, **simplified**, **not modelled** or **unverified**. Do not infer fidelity or absence from the existence of a virtual aircraft/weapon.

## Editorial disposition

Tighten repetition; preserve distinguishing technical explanations, source caveats and cross-links. Do not change aviation claims without SME review.
