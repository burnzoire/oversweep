# Editorial review — Weapons terms

- Source: `content/encyclopedia/terminology/weapons.md`
- Reviewed: 2026-11-02
- Scope: Inspected glossary terms and reference structure, without rendering the site or independent SME validation
- Scope: 38 glossary entries

## 1. Is it too long?

Thirty-eight definitions are reasonable. T-45 training-specific sight terms should be separated from broadly applicable ordnance terminology rather than abbreviated away.

## 2. Imagery and interactivity

A lookup glossary needs linkable terms and clear aliases. Weapons Delivery already provides diagrams and quizzes: cross-link instead of duplicating interactive content.

## 3. Learning outcomes

Distinguish fuzing, arming, guidance, sighting and release geometry vocabulary and understand use of CCIP and ballistic terms.

For a terminology page the outcome is rapid, correct recognition and disambiguation, not completion of a narrative lesson.

## 4. Missing important information

Some definitions derive from T-45 training examples and must be labelled by aircraft/application; clarify modern computed release modes versus older manual sights.

## 5. Simulator relevance and differences

DCS/BMS weapon fuze, sight, CCIP, fragmentation and delivery mode behaviour can be simplified or unsupported by aircraft; add scoped simulator notes only with evidence.

Explain applicability to specific simulator/module/version only where verified. Classify implemented, simplified, unavailable or unverified, and keep documented real-world terminology distinct from simulation features. Use existing sim-panel styling where applicable.

## Recommended action

Improve term discoverability, usage context and cross-links before trimming authoritative definitions. SME source review remains required for doctrinal changes.

**Applied (record revision, 2026-11-05):** the set now links the Weapons Delivery module, and its intro says that T-45 numbers in a definition are the trainer's. No sim note added.
