# Encyclopedia Aircraft revision 1 — European and Asian jets, C-130J evidence

Claim-to-source notes for `content/encyclopedia/aircraft/` records AJS 37
Viggen, Mirage 2000C, Mirage F1, JF-17, C-101, MB-339, L-39 and C-130J. The
page template and conventions are described in
`docs/reviews/encyclopedia-aircraft-us-jets-evidence.md`. These notes are
review evidence, not instructions to the reviewer.

## Modelling choices

- **Viggen**: the record describes the AJS 37 but the Wikipedia specification
  block is for the JA 37 fighter (RM8B engine), so the plate labels speed,
  ceiling, range and thrust as JA 37 figures.
- **Mirage F1 ceiling**: Wikipedia 20,000 m and FAS 52,000 ft are both shown;
  the quick table uses 20,000 m (65,600 ft).
- **C-130J** is listed with propeller aircraft (turboprop). Its 92-troop and
  64-paratroop capacities are FAS figures for the C-130 family.
- **Unit conversions** for the quick table: km/h ÷ 1.852 = kt; m × 3.2808 = ft,
  rounded to the nearest 100 ft.

## Known gaps

- No current manufacturer or national air force data sheets were read.
- Swedish, French, Spanish and Italian official sources were not consulted.
