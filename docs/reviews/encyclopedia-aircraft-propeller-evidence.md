# Encyclopedia Aircraft revision 1 — propeller aircraft evidence

Claim-to-source notes for `content/encyclopedia/aircraft/` records P-51D,
P-47D, F4U-1D, Spitfire LF Mk IX, Mosquito FB Mk VI, Bf 109 K-4, Fw 190 A-8,
Fw 190 D-9, I-16, La-7, Yak-52 and Christen Eagle II. Template and conventions:
`docs/reviews/encyclopedia-aircraft-us-jets-evidence.md`. These notes are review
evidence, not instructions to the reviewer.

## Narrative text and photographs

- Record bodies are written as narrative history, following the earlier
  batches. Every paragraph ends with its citation; quotations (Kartveli, Tank,
  Göring, Gorbatyuk, Eric Brown) are as the cited article gives them.
- Where the sources differ both are shown: P-51 first flight (26 October 1940
  in Wikipedia, 25 October in the museum record); Bf 109 first flight (29 May
  1935, September 1935); F4U and Fw 190 production totals; I-16 Type 24 engine
  power (1,100 hp in the specification, 900 hp in the text); La-7 retirement
  (1947 in the text, 1952 in the infobox).
- Photographs follow the earlier batches: Wikimedia Commons, public-domain
  government photographs where available, otherwise CC BY or CC BY-SA with a
  licence link; captions from the Commons file description.

## Modelling choices

- **Variant figures**: F4U-1D ceiling and range are F4U-4 figures; Mosquito
  FB VI ceiling and range are B Mk XVI figures; Bf 109 K-4 ceiling and range are
  G-6 figures. Each is labelled on the plate.
- **Spitfire LF Mk IX ceiling** is not given in the sources read; the plate
  quotes the 1942 trials report instead, and the quick table shows "not given".
- **Fw 190 D-9** ceiling and range are not given in the sources read. Its speed
  (709 km/h) is from the museum record; the altitude printed beside it there
  is internally inconsistent and is not used.
- **Christen Eagle II** is a civilian sport aircraft and is labelled as such.
- **Unit conversions**: km/h ÷ 1.852 = kt; mph ÷ 1.15078 = kt; m × 3.2808 = ft,
  rounded to 100 ft.
