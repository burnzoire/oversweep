## 1. Length and concision

Four narratives (fleet defence, swing wings/Phoenix, Gulf of Sidra, Iran) are useful, but the opening procurement history can be compressed. Put fleet-defence design and crew/radar/missile relationship ahead of the F-111B story. Distinguish F-14A/B variants rather than treating every period as one configuration.

## 2. Imagery and interactivity

Two photos exist. An annotated AWG-9/Phoenix engagement concept or variable-sweep planform illustration would teach more than a third aircraft photograph; static is sufficient.

**Decision:** Do not add interactions solely to increase engagement. Confirm the existing photos, data plate and responsive layout before commissioning visuals. Any new illustration must fit the current Oversweep visual system and have a verified rights/source basis.

## 3. Learning outcomes

Explain why fleet defence drove the F-14 design; describe the radar/Phoenix partnership; distinguish sweep-wing advantages from tactical limitations; identify A/B configuration differences.

These are the proposed reader outcomes; use them to check whether the page's headings and explanations serve a purpose, not to introduce training-module-style quizzes into encyclopedia records.

## 4. Missing or insufficiently prominent content

Iranian combat-loss and 2025–26 destruction figures require dated independent verification; improve radar/crew-role context and link directly to Case III and AIM-54.

## 5. Flight simulation: implemented, simplified, absent, unknown

DCS F-14A/B and available B(U) distinctions need module/version specificity; check Jester/RIO, radar modes, AIM-54 behaviour and carrier integrations, not assume the historical AWG-9 is fully recreated.

**Verification required:** Identify relevant simulator, aircraft/module and version, then record **modelled**, **simplified**, **not modelled** or **unverified** with documentation or reproducible observation. Absence and fidelity cannot be inferred from the real-world source. Present validated simulator-specific notes using the established `.sim-panel` treatment where supported; keep real-world description separate.

Prioritise the factual distinctions above. Preserve useful technical reference details and cross-links, while removing redundant narrative before adding new content. Changes to technical claims require independent aviation SME evidence review.
