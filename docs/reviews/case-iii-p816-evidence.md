## Weather conflict — p. 2-4

> Case III weather is any ceiling below 1,000 feet or a visibility less than 5NM.

Mapping: Compare recovery-section text below; do not teach one numerical threshold as universal.

## Weather conflict — p. 2-22

> The Case III recovery is used for all night operations, as well as during the day when the weather is below Case II minimums (less than 1,000-3).

Mapping: Internal disagreement with p. 2-4 explains omitted minima.

## Holding — p. 2-20

> The holding pattern is a six-minute left-hand pattern.

Mapping: A historical source pattern, not a universal holding rule.

## Turn/leg timing — p. 2-20

> Two- minute turns and one-minute legs are normally used for the pattern.

Mapping: Two two-minute turns and two one-minute legs total six minutes.

## Timing discrepancy — p. 2-20

> If unable to do this, notify Marshal so that timing adjustments to the landing interval can be made.

Mapping: Report inability to meet assigned fix timing; does not authorise arbitrary clearance changes.

## Case III incorporation — p. 2-23

Mapping: Links preceding holding description to Case III.

## Profile — p. 2-23

Mapping: Historical CV-1 example; no aircraft limit is inferred.

## Platform — p. 2-23

> At 5,000 feet (platform), the rate of descent will be reduced to 2,000 feet per minute.

Mapping: Platform is an altitude checkpoint.
