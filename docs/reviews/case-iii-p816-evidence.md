## G3 — Jet/turboprop marshal

Source: “Jet/Turboprop Marshal”, immediately preceding Table 11-11,
printed **p. 11-33**.
[Readable source](https://navyaviation.tpub.com/14342A/Table-11-11-Standard-Cv-1-Marshal-Pattern-389.html).

Selected source sentence (10 words):

> The holding pattern is a left-hand 6-minute racetrack pattern.

Original paraphrase: the fix is normally behind the expected final bearing;
its assigned distance and altitude depend on the pattern. The inbound leg passes
over the fix. This independently corroborates that the six-minute left-hand
pattern is not unique to the T-45C publication. It does not establish one
universal speed or unrestricted authority to extend legs. P-816 supplies its
own two-minute-turn/one-minute-leg example separately.
