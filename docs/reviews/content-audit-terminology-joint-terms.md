# Editorial review — Joint terms and abbreviations

- Source: `content/encyclopedia/terminology/joint-terms.md`
- Reviewed: 2026-11-02
- Scope: Inspected glossary terms and reference structure, without rendering the site or independent SME validation
- Scope: 49 glossary entries

## 1. Is it too long?

Forty-nine entries represent lookup material; don't compress individual formal definitions without source review. Reduce unnecessary repeated expansions and notes.

## 2. Imagery and interactivity

No image required for a glossary, and not a candidate for mini-games. Search, anchors and disambiguation have priority.

## 3. Learning outcomes

Differentiate CAS versus air interdiction, DCA versus OCA, EW/EA and related cross-service vocabulary.

For a terminology page the outcome is rapid, correct recognition and disambiguation, not completion of a narrative lesson.

## 4. Missing important information

Formal doctrinal language changes by edition and service; scope DoD definitions and don't turn broad terms into operational procedures. Review duplicate terms appearing in other sets.

## 5. Simulator relevance and differences

In sims, real-world command-and-control, planning and electronic warfare functions may be absent or simplified. Mark terms as doctrine, not automatically executable in DCS/BMS.

Explain applicability to specific simulator/module/version only where verified. Classify implemented, simplified, unavailable or unverified, and keep documented real-world terminology distinct from simulation features. Use existing sim-panel styling where applicable.

## Recommended action

Improve term discoverability, usage context and cross-links before trimming authoritative definitions. SME source review remains required for doctrinal changes.

**Applied (record revision, 2026-11-05):** the set now links the Close Air Support and SEAD and DEAD modules. The JP 1-02 edition note and service-specific senses were already in place. No sim note added.
