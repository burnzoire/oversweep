## 1. Is it too long?

Short reference set. No length issue. Some definitions are dense because they distinguish navigation systems; keep this precision.

## 2. Imagery and interactivity

No new images required; direct links to HSI/TACAN, radial and DME interactive illustrations in Air Navigation are more beneficial.

## 3. Learning outcomes

Distinguish heading/track/drift, relative bearing versus radial, slant range versus ground distance, variation versus deviation.

For a terminology page the outcome is rapid, correct recognition and disambiguation, not completion of a narrative lesson.

## 4. Missing important information

The dual meaning of 'drift' is explicitly noted; check that navigation illustrations cover both. Define reference frame and units for numerical examples.

## 5. Simulator relevance and differences

DCS/BMS TACAN/VOR/GPS/INS capabilities vary by aircraft and map. Terms like RAIM may be real-world only depending on specific module/version; don't assume availability.

Explain applicability to specific simulator/module/version only where verified. Classify implemented, simplified, unavailable or unverified, and keep documented real-world terminology distinct from simulation features. Use existing sim-panel styling where applicable.

## Recommended action

Improve term discoverability, usage context and cross-links before trimming authoritative definitions. SME source review remains required for doctrinal changes.
