## Coverage

Falcon 4.0: Allied Force, released by Lead Pursuit in 2005, is built on the source code of the 1998 Falcon 4.0 and simulates the Block 50/52 F-16 in a series of missions in the Balkans. It added the Balkans to Falcon's original Korean battlefield, with a dynamic campaign that keeps running in real time whether or not the player is flying. [[1]](#source-1) Lead Pursuit was deregistered in 2011; Benchmark Sims carries the code on in Falcon BMS, which has its own [Balkans theatre](record:bms-balkans). [[1]](#source-1)
