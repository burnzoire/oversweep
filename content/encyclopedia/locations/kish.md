## Role today

Kish International is the entry point for the hundreds of thousands of tourists who visit Kish Island, and foreigners arriving from abroad get 14 days' visa-free entry under a scheme separate from mainland Iran's. [[1]](#source-1) It hosts the biennial Iran Kish Air Show. [[1]](#source-1) In March 2026, during the Iran war, Iran International reported a military strike on the airport. [[1]](#source-1)

## History

Before the 1979 revolution Iran had ordered two Concordes for Kish–Paris and Kish–London routes, and the airport was designed to handle them. [[1]](#source-1) In the 1980s the Kish Free Zone Organisation offered visa-free travel and tax breaks to compete with Dubai, but the plan failed and the island became mainly a resort for Iranians. [[1]](#source-1) In 2015, 2.7 million passengers used the airport. [[1]](#source-1)

## The airfield

Two parallel asphalt runways, 09L/27R and 09R/27L, each 3,659 m long. [[1]](#source-1) Source 2 lists 09L/27R as closed. [[2]](#source-2)
