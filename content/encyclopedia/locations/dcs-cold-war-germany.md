## Coverage

The map is in early access. It recreates central Europe in the 1980s, with more than 100 military airfields and heliports such as Ramstein, Spangdahlem, Hahn, Bitburg and Sembach, large civil airports such as Tegel, Schönefeld, Tempelhof, Frankfurt, Hamburg and Hannover, and army bases, listening posts, naval bases, training grounds, weapons stores and the fortified border between East and West. [[1]](#source-1) The first release concentrates on central Germany with summer textures; later updates are to add detail to the north and south, extend the map west to the Netherlands, Belgium and Denmark, and add spring, autumn and winter. [[1]](#source-1)
