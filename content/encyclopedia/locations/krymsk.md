## Role today

Krymsk is a Russian Aerospace Forces fighter base, in the 4th Air and Air Defence Forces Army of the Southern Military District. [[1]](#source-1) As of February 2022 it held the headquarters of the 1st Guards Composite Aviation Division and the 3rd Guards Composite Aviation Regiment, with two squadrons of upgraded Su-27s. [[1]](#source-1) The Russian article describes the unit there now as an aviation group of the 7th Aerospace Forces Brigade, flying Su-27SM3s, Su-30M2s, Su-27s, Ka-27 helicopters and An-2s. [[2]](#source-2) On the night of 14 November 2024 Ukraine attacked the base with drones; Russia's Ministry of Defence said 51 were shot down and no one was hurt. [[1]](#source-1)

## History

The base's fighter regiment absorbed the 562nd Fighter Regiment on 9 January 2001 to become the 3rd Guards Fighter Regiment, and was renamed the 6972nd Guards Aviation Base on 12 January 2009. [[1]](#source-1) The airfield closed for rebuilding in 2011. [[1]](#source-1) During the Krymsk floods of July 2012, *Izvestiya* quoted an eyewitness saying about 15 combat aircraft were flooded; the Ministry of Defence denied serious damage, saying the field stands on high ground and the water there rose only 10 cm. [[1]](#source-1) [[2]](#source-2) Afterwards 5,500 troops were sent to help rebuild the town, with a tent camp on the airfield. [[1]](#source-1)

## The airfield

A single concrete runway, 04/22, now 3,000 m long and 60 m wide. [[2]](#source-2) [[3]](#source-3)
