## Role today

Bandar Lengeh Airport is a civil and military airport, run by the Iran Airports Company and used by Iranian Navy aviation. [[1]](#source-1) It has only domestic flights, to Iranian cities including Shiraz and Tehran. [[1]](#source-1)

## History

It was once international, with Iran Air flights to Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Muscat, but scheduled international services ended in the early 1980s. [[1]](#source-1)

## The airfield

One asphalt runway, 08/26, 2,500 m long. [[1]](#source-1)
