## Role today

Taftanaz was a Syrian Air Force helicopter base in Idlib Governorate, home to two squadrons of Mi-8/17 Hips until rebels took it in January 2013. [[1]](#source-1) OurAirports now lists it as closed. [[2]](#source-2) OpenStreetMap names the field Afis Air Base. [[3]](#source-3)

## History

Rebels led by fighters of the al-Nusra Front and an Ahrar al-Sham battalion overran the base in the second week of January 2013. [[1]](#source-1) On 6 February 2020, as government forces advanced in north-western Syria, the Turkish Armed Forces built a military post on the base; the Syrian Air Force struck it within hours, and on 2 March 2020 a government attack killed one Turkish soldier and wounded three, according to the Syrian Observatory for Human Rights. [[1]](#source-1)

## The airfield

No published runway data was found. OpenStreetMap maps two strips: one running roughly east–west, about 1,200 m long, and a shorter one of about 850 m running north-east. [[3]](#source-3)
