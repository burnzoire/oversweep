## Role today

Al Minhad is a United Arab Emirates Air Force base south of Dubai and a logistics hub for the UAE's allies. [[1]](#source-1) The Australian Defence Force runs Camp Baird there, its main transport and logistics hub in the Middle East and the headquarters of Joint Task Force 633. [[1]](#source-1) In March 2024 the Princess Royal opened Donnelly Lines, a permanent British facility with a headquarters and accommodation. [[1]](#source-1)

The UAE asks foreign militaries not to advertise the host nation or the location of their operations there, because of local sensitivities about foreign forces in the country. [[1]](#source-1)

## History

Several allied countries are believed to have used the base since the early 2000s to supply operations in Afghanistan; a 2010 US diplomatic cable called it "a critical hub for Coalition/ISAF partners in Afghanistan". [[1]](#source-1) The RAAF flew AP-3C Orions from it from 2003, and Australia made it its main logistics hub in the region after its combat forces left Iraq in 2008. [[1]](#source-1) Against the Islamic State it deployed F/A-18A Hornets, replaced in early 2015 by F/A-18F Super Hornets, with E-7A Wedgetails and KC-30As. [[1]](#source-1) The Netherlands and New Zealand also deployed forces there. [[1]](#source-1)

The RAF stood up No. 906 Expeditionary Air Wing there in January 2013. [[1]](#source-1) In Operation Pitting, the evacuation of Kabul in 2021, RAF C-17s and C-130Js shuttled between Kabul and Minhad, and Voyagers flew evacuees on to RAF Brize Norton. [[1]](#source-1)

## The airfield

One runway, 09/27, 3,932 m long and surfaced in asphalt and concrete, with a parallel taxiway. [[1]](#source-1)
