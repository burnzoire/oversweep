## Coverage

DCS: Marianas is a free map of the archipelago in the present day, around Andersen Air Force Base, the key US strategic asset in the western Pacific. [[1]](#source-1) It has large airfields on each of the main islands, with the expansive Andersen and the airfields of Rota, Tinian and Saipan, and large expanses of ocean for carrier and naval operations. [[1]](#source-1) Each airfield's radios, runways and runway beacons are in the game's terrain data, which the airfield records here compare with the real fields. [[2]](#source-2)
