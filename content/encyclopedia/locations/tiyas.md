## Role today

Tiyas, known as T-4, is Syria's largest air base, in Homs Governorate west of Palmyra. [[1]](#source-1) Members of the Free Syrian Army captured it in early December 2024. [[1]](#source-1) Turkey then began working to take control of it and was preparing to deploy Hisar or SIPER air defence systems there, according to *Middle East Eye*'s sources, and the base was later bombed by the Israeli Air Force. [[1]](#source-1)

## History

In the 1970s and 1980s the Soviet Union had access to Tiyas for periodic deployments of naval aircraft. [[1]](#source-1) Its squadrons have included two of MiG-25s, one of Su-24MK2s and one of Su-22M4s. [[1]](#source-1)

In the civil war the Syrian Air Force and Iran's Quds Force flew operations from it against the Islamic State and the opposition. [[1]](#source-1) The Islamic State attacked it in December 2016, a day after taking Palmyra, and claimed to have destroyed four aircraft. [[1]](#source-1) Israel struck it on 10 February 2018, destroying the main observation tower according to the Syrian Observatory for Human Rights, and again, reportedly, on 9 April 2018, destroying a drone hangar and an Iranian-supplied Tor system before it was operational. [[1]](#source-1) Iran-backed militias launched drone strikes from it against US bases in northern Syria in summer 2021, and Israel struck it again on 8 October 2021, saying Iranian forces still used it. [[1]](#source-1)
