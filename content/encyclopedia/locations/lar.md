## Role today

Larestan International Airport is near Lar, at an elevation of about 800 m. [[1]](#source-1) It is run by the Iran Airports Company. [[1]](#source-1)

## History

The airport began operations in 1983, mostly with domestic flights, and became international after the Gulf War with a first international flight to Dubai. [[1]](#source-1)

## The airfield

One asphalt runway, 09/27, 3,329 m long. [[1]](#source-1)
