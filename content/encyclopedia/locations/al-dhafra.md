## Role today

Al Dhafra is a United Arab Emirates Air Force base south of Abu Dhabi and the headquarters of its Western Air Command. [[1]](#source-1) Its Fighter Wing flies F-16E/F Desert Falcons with three Shaheen squadrons and Mirage 2000-9s with 71 and 76 Squadrons. [[1]](#source-1)

It is also a major base for foreign air forces. The US Air Force's 380th Air Expeditionary Wing has been there since January 2002, flying reconnaissance, airborne command and control, aerial refuelling and fighter missions for the campaign against the Islamic State and earlier for NATO operations in Afghanistan. [[1]](#source-1) A US Army Patriot battalion defends it. [[1]](#source-1) France opened its own settlement in the north-west corner in September 2008, and since June 2016 Escadron de Chasse 1/7 Provence has flown Rafales from it. [[1]](#source-1)

## History

The base was built in 1983. [[1]](#source-1) US forces have been there since the early 1990s, but the US Air Force only acknowledged it officially in August 2017. [[1]](#source-1) The 380th wing has flown F-15C/Es, F-22As, KC-10As, E-3 Sentries, U-2Ss and Global Hawks, and the first US F-35As deployed to the Middle East arrived there in April 2019. [[1]](#source-1)

In January 2022 the Houthis fired missiles at the base: an Emirati THAAD battery intercepted a medium-range ballistic missile on 17 January, the system's first known combat use, and Patriots destroyed two more on 24 January. [[1]](#source-1) A missile hit the base during the 2026 Iran war and caused damage. [[1]](#source-1)

## The airfield

Two parallel asphalt runways, 13L/31R and 13R/31L, each 3,661 m long. [[1]](#source-1)
