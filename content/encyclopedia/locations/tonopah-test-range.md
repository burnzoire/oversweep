## Role today

Tonopah Test Range Airport is an Air Combat Command airfield on the Tonopah Test Range, at the north-west of the Nellis ranges. [[1]](#source-1) [[2]](#source-2) Its main known use is shuttling government workers to the range from Las Vegas, and the Department of Energy runs about 15 flights a week from it. [[1]](#source-1) The surviving F-117A fleet has been stored in its hangars since 2008, and a detachment of the 53rd Test and Evaluation Group may use it for foreign aircraft testing. [[1]](#source-1)

## History

The airfield opened in 1957 for the Atomic Energy Commission's nuclear weapons work on the range. [[1]](#source-1) In the late 1970s, as the collection of Soviet aircraft at [Groom Lake](record:groom-lake) outgrew that base, the Air Force moved it here. [[1]](#source-1) The 4477th Test and Evaluation Squadron flew MiG-17s, [MiG-21s](record:mig-21), MiG-23s and other foreign types against US aircrew in the Constant Peg programme: more than 15,000 [sorties](term:sortie) between 1980 and the end of flying in March 1988. [[1]](#source-1) The base was not publicly reported as an Air Force airfield until 1985. [[1]](#source-1)

From October 1979 the field was rebuilt for the F-117A, with a longer runway, a control tower, arresting gear, new navigation aids and the first 24 aircraft hangars, at a cost of over $100 million. [[1]](#source-1) The 4450th Tactical Group moved here from Groom Lake from May 1982 and flew the jets only at night until the Air Force acknowledged them in November 1988. [[1]](#source-1) The 37th Tactical Fighter Wing took over in October 1989 and sent F-117s from here to Panama in December 1989 and to Saudi Arabia for the Gulf War in August 1990. [[1]](#source-1) The wing moved to Holloman AFB in 1992, and by August that year the airfield was largely in caretaker status. [[1]](#source-1)

## The airfield

One 11,998 ft concrete runway, 15/33, with ILS approaches to both ends and approach lights on runway 15. [[2]](#source-2) The FAA notes heavy military operations in the surrounding restricted area and a migratory bird hazard from October to March. [[2]](#source-2)
