## Role today

Damascus International is Syria's busiest airport and the home base of Syrian Air. [[1]](#source-1) It closed during the opposition offensive on Damascus on 8 December 2024 and reopened to domestic flights on 18 December; international flights resumed on 7 January 2025 with a Qatar Airways arrival from Doha. [[1]](#source-1) Since then Turkey has helped renovate it and installed a new radar in January 2026, and in August 2025 Syria and Qatar signed a US$4 billion deal for a third terminal. [[1]](#source-1)

## History

French companies began building the airport in 1965, and it replaced the old Mezzeh military airfield as the city's main airport. [[1]](#source-1) By the 1980s more than 30 airlines served it. [[1]](#source-1) The civil war closed it and its approach roads on and off, and most foreign airlines left. [[1]](#source-1)

In the later war it was repeatedly struck by Israel, which said it was hitting Iranian weapons transfers. [[1]](#source-1) A strike in June 2022 damaged the runways and halted flights for two weeks; another on 2 January 2023 killed at least two soldiers. [[1]](#source-1) In October and November 2023, during the Gaza war, strikes on 12 October, 22 October and 26 November each put it out of service again, the second together with Aleppo's airport. [[1]](#source-1)

## The airfield

Two parallel runways, 05R/23L and 05L/23R, able to take the largest airliners in service. [[1]](#source-1) Source 2 lists 05R/23L as 11,811 ft long and 05L/23R as closed. [[2]](#source-2) The southern part of the airport has hardened aircraft shelters and artillery revetments. [[1]](#source-1)
