## Role today

Rota International is the public airport of Rota, owned by the Commonwealth Ports Authority and used mostly for short commuter flights; Star Marianas Air connects it with [Guam](record:guam) and [Saipan](record:saipan). [[1]](#source-1) In the year to March 2006 it handled about 17 aircraft movements a day, 94% of them air taxi, 4% general aviation and 2% military. [[1]](#source-1)

## History

The Japanese built a single runway here during the Second World War, and US bombing put it out of action. [[1]](#source-1) After the Marines took control of the island, 300 men of the US Navy's 48th Naval Construction Battalion made the airfield usable again in September and October 1945 and lengthened the runway, which then served as an emergency landing strip for the Tinian and Saipan airfields. [[1]](#source-1)

## The airfield

One asphalt runway, 09/27, 7,000 ft long and 150 ft wide, with an NDB on the field. [[2]](#source-2) There is no control tower; Rota air/ground radio works traffic on 123.6 MHz. [[2]](#source-2)
