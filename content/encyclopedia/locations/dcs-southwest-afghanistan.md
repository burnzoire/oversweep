## Coverage

Southwest Afghanistan was the first region of the [DCS: Afghanistan](record:dcs-afghanistan) map to reach early access, and it can be bought on its own. [[1]](#source-1) Its twelve detailed airfields at launch were Kandahar, Herat, Bost, Camp Bastion, Chaghcharan, Dwyer, Farah, Maymana Zahiraddin Faryabi, Qala i Naw, Shindand, Tirinkot and Zaranj. [[1]](#source-1) Owning only this region still allows online play across the whole map, with the other regions in lower detail. [[1]](#source-1)
