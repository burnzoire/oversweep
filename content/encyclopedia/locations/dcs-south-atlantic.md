## Coverage

The map covers more than 3.1 million km² of Argentina, Chile and the Falkland Islands, built from high-resolution aerial and satellite imagery, from the Andes and the plains of Patagonia to the Falklands and Stanley. [[1]](#source-1) Its terrain data lists 26 airfields. In the Falklands they include Port Stanley, Mount Pleasant, a San Carlos forward operating base and Goose Green; on the mainland, Argentine fields such as Río Grande, Río Gallegos and San Julián and Chilean ones such as Punta Arenas.
