## Role today

Sirri Island's airport is uncontrolled, with a single runway, though Iranian military aircraft have been seen there. [[1]](#source-1) The island, 17.3 km² of it, is an oil centre: the Iranian Offshore Oil Company runs gas and crude oil projects there, and the Sirri district includes the Sivand, Dena, Nosrat, Alvand and Esfand oil fields. [[1]](#source-1)

## History

On 18 April 1988, in Operation Praying Mantis, US naval forces destroyed an Iranian oil platform at Sirri; it was rebuilt after the war. [[1]](#source-1)

## The airfield

One asphalt runway, 12/30, listed at 8,345 ft. [[2]](#source-2)
