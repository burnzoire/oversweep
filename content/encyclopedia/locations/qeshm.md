## Role today

Qeshm International is the airport of Qeshm Island, with flights to the United Arab Emirates and to Tehran. [[1]](#source-1) In 2004 about 73% of its passengers were international, around half of them on quick turnaround "visa" flights for migrant workers in the UAE. [[1]](#source-1) It has no cargo facilities. [[1]](#source-1)

## History

Most of the airport was built in 1996 and 1997, and it opened to commercial traffic in July 1997; the fuel farm and control tower followed in 2002–03. [[1]](#source-1) It has recorded some of the highest dew points ever measured, 97 °F on 28 August 2024 and 31 July 2025. [[1]](#source-1)

## The airfield

One asphalt runway, 05/23, 4,226 m long. [[1]](#source-1) Only a small area on the north-west side of the large site is used for aviation. [[1]](#source-1)
