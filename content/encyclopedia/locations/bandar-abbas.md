## Role today

Bandar Abbas International is the airport of Bandar Abbas, with flights across Iran and to the Gulf states; about 1.3 million passengers used it in 2017. [[1]](#source-1) It is a joint civil and military airfield, used by the Iran Airports Company, the Iranian Air Force, Iranian Navy aviation and the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps. [[1]](#source-1)

## History

Iran Air Flight 655 was shot down in 1988 after taking off from Bandar Abbas. [[1]](#source-1) An Iranian Air Force F-4 Phantom II crashed near the city on 14 October 2012, killing its two crew. [[1]](#source-1)

## The airfield

Two parallel asphalt runways: 03R/21L, 3,660 m long, and 03L/21R, 3,457 m. [[1]](#source-1) It can take widebody aircraft such as the Boeing 747 and 777. [[1]](#source-1)
