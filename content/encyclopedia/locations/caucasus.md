## The Caucasus and the eastern Black Sea

### Historical significance

In the Second World War the region was the prize of Germany's 1942 summer offensive. German troops took Rostov-on-Don on 25 July 1942, opening the way to the oil fields at Maykop, Grozny and Baku, and Operation Edelweiss carried them as far as Alagir and Ordzhonikidze by early November before the threat of encirclement forced them back that winter. [[1]](#source-1)

After the Soviet Union broke up, Georgia lost control of two regions. In the 1992–1993 war in Abkhazia, Abkhaz separatists, backed by North Caucasian volunteers and by Russian forces stationed in and near Abkhazia, defeated Georgian government forces; after Sukhumi fell on 27 September 1993, about 250,000 Georgians were displaced. [[2]](#source-2) In August 2008 Russia invaded Georgia in support of South Ossetia and Abkhazia, in what is regarded as the first European war of the 21st century. [[3]](#source-3) Russian aircraft bombed Gori, the port of Poti and the Vaziani base outside Tbilisi, the Black Sea Fleet blockaded the Georgian coast, and Georgian air defences, notably SA-11 Buk launchers, were credited by US officials with shooting down a Tu-22M bomber; six Russian aircraft were shot down in all. [[3]](#source-3)

### Forces deployed

- **Vaziani**, south-east of Tbilisi: built as a Soviet Army base in 1941 and held by Russia until 2001, it is now home to the Georgian Land Forces' 4th Mechanized Brigade and 23rd Infantry Battalion, beside a former Soviet airfield still in limited use. [[4]](#source-4) Georgia reported that two Russian bombs hit it on 8 August 2008. [[3]](#source-3) From 2011 it hosted the Agile Spirit and Noble Partner exercises with US and NATO forces, which stopped when Georgia's military ties with the West were suspended in 2024. [[4]](#source-4)
- **Mozdok**, North Ossetia: a Russian air base with a detachment of the 485th Independent Helicopter Regiment. [[5]](#source-5)
- **Krymsk**, Krasnodar Krai: a Russian Aerospace Forces base of the 4th Air and Air Defence Forces Army, Southern Military District. [[6]](#source-6)
- **Black Sea Fleet**: two battalions of its marines took part in the 2008 war, and its ships blockaded Georgia's coast from 10 August, the fleet's first such operation since 1945. [[3]](#source-3)
