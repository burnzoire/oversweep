## Role today

Anapa is the airport of the Black Sea resort of Anapa, run by Basel Aero, and a joint-use airfield that Russian Ministry of Defence aircraft use from time to time. [[1]](#source-1) [[2]](#source-2) All civil flights have been suspended since Russia invaded Ukraine on 24 February 2022. [[1]](#source-1) [[2]](#source-2)

## History

Anapa had scheduled flights from Krasnodar from 1934, first from a field on the town's high shore. [[1]](#source-1) [[2]](#source-2) In 1969 work began on a new airfield near Vityazevo; at the Air Force's insistence it was given a 2,500 m runway, and because military aircraft were to be based there the press was forbidden to mention it. [[2]](#source-2) The first regular passenger flight landed on 15 April 1974, and on 11 May the first Moscow flight brought a group of NATO military attachés to the resort. [[2]](#source-2) The airport was formally opened in June 1976. [[1]](#source-1) [[2]](#source-2)

On 9 September 1976 an An-24 and a Yak-40 collided over Anapa after an air traffic controller's error, killing 70 people. [[1]](#source-1) On 15 March 1982 a Black Sea Fleet An-26 crashed near Anapa, killing all eight aboard. [[2]](#source-2) The runway was rebuilt from 2011, and a new terminal opened on 12 July 2017. [[1]](#source-1) [[2]](#source-2)

## The airfield

A single concrete runway, 04/22, 2,500 m long. [[1]](#source-1) [[3]](#source-3)
