## Role today

Jirah, sometimes spelled al-Jarrah, is a small Syrian Air Force base. [[1]](#source-1) The Syrian Democratic Forces took it when the Syrian army collapsed in the 2024 offensive, and withdrew east of the Euphrates on 17 January 2026 during an offensive by the transitional government's forces. [[1]](#source-1) Its use since then is not described in the sources consulted.

## History

Opposition fighters of Ahrar al-Sham and the Free Syrian Army surrounded the base in mid-January 2013 and captured it on 12 February. [[1]](#source-1) The Islamic State held it from January 2014 until the Syrian army retook it on 12 May 2017, in the Maskanah Plains offensive, securing it fully by 29 May. [[1]](#source-1) It reopened in January 2023, with a Syrian air defence unit stationed there. [[1]](#source-1)

## The airfield

One asphalt runway, 10/28, listed at 10,180 ft by 130 ft. [[2]](#source-2)
