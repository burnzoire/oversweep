## Role today

Hama Air Base lies west of the city of Hama. [[1]](#source-1) The Syrian Air Force used it heavily through the civil war, until opposition forces led by Hayat Tahrir al-Sham took it on 5 December 2024 when government forces pulled out of Hama. [[1]](#source-1) Its use since then is not described in the sources consulted.

## History

On 18 May 2018 a series of large explosions at the base reportedly killed 11 people. [[1]](#source-1) Syrian officials blamed a technical failure in the depots; other accounts blamed an Israeli strike or sabotage by the jihadist group Saraya al-Jihad, which claimed it. [[1]](#source-1) On 1 March 2020, according to the Syrian Observatory for Human Rights, Turkish drones bombed the 47th Brigade based there. [[1]](#source-1)

## The airfield

One paved runway, 2,783 m long. [[1]](#source-1) Source 2 lists it as 09/27, concrete, 150 ft wide. [[2]](#source-2)
