## Role today

Fujairah International serves Fujairah City. [[1]](#source-1) One terminal handles both passengers and cargo, with capacity for 2 million passengers a year, and the airport is home to the Fujairah Aviation Academy and does aircraft dismantling and maintenance. [[1]](#source-1)

## History

The airport began operations in October 1987. [[1]](#source-1) A 2014 memorandum with Abu Dhabi Airports started a masterplan for the airfield and terminal, and on 3 December 2022 a new runway, more than 3 km long and 45 m wide, opened. [[1]](#source-1)

## The airfield

The infobox gives runway 11/29 as 3,749 m of asphalt; the 2022 runway is more than 3 km long. [[1]](#source-1)
