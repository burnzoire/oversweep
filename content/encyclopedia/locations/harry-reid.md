## Role today

Harry Reid International is the airport of Las Vegas and the Las Vegas Valley, owned by Clark County and run by its Department of Aviation. [[1]](#source-1) More than 58.4 million passengers used it in 2024, and it is an operating base for Allegiant Air, Frontier Airlines, JSX and Southwest Airlines. [[1]](#source-1) Janet Air flights to secret military sites leave from their own terminal building here. [[1]](#source-1)

## History

Flight instructor George Crockett built Alamo Field on the site in 1942, and it opened in January 1943 with three gravel runways; at the time the airlines used the Las Vegas Army Airfield, now [Nellis Air Force Base](record:nellis). [[1]](#source-1) When the military wanted that base back for the Cold War, Clark County bought Crockett's field, and it reopened as McCarran Field on 19 December 1948, named for Senator Pat McCarran. [[1]](#source-1) Traffic grew with the Las Vegas casinos, from 36,000 passengers in 1948 to nearly a million in 1959; a new terminal opened in 1963 and Terminal 3 in 2012. [[1]](#source-1) The county renamed the airport for Senator Harry Reid in December 2021. [[1]](#source-1)

## The airfield

Four runways in two parallel pairs: 08L/26R, 14,835 ft long, and 08R/26L, 10,526 ft, running east–west, and 01L/19R and 01R/19L, about 9,770 ft each, running north–south. [[2]](#source-2) The FAA warns of sun glare reflected from glass hotels north-west of the airport and of large numbers of birds and bats around the field between sunset and sunrise. [[2]](#source-2)
