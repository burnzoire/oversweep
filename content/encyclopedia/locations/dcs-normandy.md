## Coverage

Normandy 2.0 covers large parts of southern England and northern France, including London and Paris, with the cities and ports on both sides of the Channel. It is built for the battles of Operation Overlord, from Omaha Beach and the Atlantic Wall to Operation Cobra, the Falaise pocket and the liberation of Paris. [[1]](#source-1) Ugra Media promises 90 airfields, from the Allied landing grounds to Farnborough, Chailey, Ford, Heathrow, Lonrai, Cormeilles-en-Vexin and Villacoublay. [[1]](#source-1) The terrain data lists 89, among them the Normandy advanced landing grounds at Saint-Pierre-du-Mont, Cricqueville-en-Bessin and Cardonville and RAF stations such as Tangmere, Biggin Hill and Manston. [[2]](#source-2)

Because of wartime blackouts, airfields, ports and cities are only lit if the mission is set in 1946. [[1]](#source-1) The older Normandy 1944 map, now marked as a deprecated version, covered 267 × 348 km and 30 airfields for the period after D-Day in late June 1944. [[3]](#source-3)
