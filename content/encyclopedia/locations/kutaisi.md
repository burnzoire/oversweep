## Role today

Kutaisi is Georgia's second-busiest airport, with 1.7 million passengers in 2024, run by the state-owned United Airports of Georgia and built up around low-cost airlines, Wizz Air above all. [[1]](#source-1) It lies beside the main road between Kutaisi and Batumi, and since 2022 trains between Tbilisi and Batumi stop at the nearby Kopitnari station. [[1]](#source-1)

## History

Before it was a civil airport it was Kopitnari, a Soviet military airfield. [[2]](#source-2) The 166th Guards Ground-Attack Regiment flew Il-10s from it from 1947 until April 1954. [[2]](#source-2) From 1960 the Air Defence Forces' 167th Guards Fighter Regiment was based there with MiG-17s and Su-9s until it was disbanded in 1976, and from 1961 the 143rd Bomber Regiment flew Il-28s, then Yak-28s from 1966 and Su-24Ms, until it was withdrawn to Morozovsk in Russia in 1993. [[2]](#source-2)

The airport closed for rebuilding in November 2011 and reopened on 27 September 2012, with Wizz Air flying in from Kyiv on the first day; in February 2016 Wizz Air announced a base there. [[1]](#source-1) [[2]](#source-2)

## The airfield

One runway, 07/25, 2,500 m long and paved in asphalt. [[1]](#source-1)
