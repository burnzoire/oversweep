## Historical significance

Since the 1970s Greece and Turkey have disputed sovereignty and rights in the Aegean Sea: territorial waters, national airspace, the role of flight information regions in controlling military flights, and the demilitarised status of some Greek islands. The dispute twice brought the two countries close to war, in 1987 and early in 1996. [[1]](#source-1) The 1996 crisis was over the islets of Imia. [[2]](#source-2) On 20 July 1974 Turkey invaded Cyprus; the invasion ended in the island's partition along the UN-monitored Green Line, which still divides it. [[3]](#source-3)

## Forces deployed

- **Souda Bay**, Crete: the Hellenic Navy's second-largest naval base and NATO's largest and most prominent in the eastern Mediterranean, with the only deep-water port in southern Europe and the Mediterranean able to take supercarriers. Known to NATO as Naval Support Activity Souda Bay, it has had the US Navy's expeditionary mobile base USS Hershel "Woody" Williams deployed there since 2020. [[4]](#source-4)
- **Andravida Air Base**, Elis: home of the Hellenic Air Force's 117th Combat Wing and of the air force's Air Tactics Center. The wing was formed in May 1969 to receive the [F-4E](record:f-4) Phantom. [[5]](#source-5)
- **Larissa Air Base**, Thessaly: home of the Hellenic Air Force's 110th Combat Wing; its runways have no ILS. USAF MQ-9 Reaper drones were based there temporarily from May 2018. [[6]](#source-6)
