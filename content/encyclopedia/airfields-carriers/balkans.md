## Historical significance

NATO fought the first combat of its history over the Balkans. Operation Deny Flight began on 12 April 1993 to enforce the UN no-fly zone over Bosnia and Herzegovina and grew to include close air support for UN troops and air strikes. On 28 February 1994, USAF F-16s shot down four of six Serb J-21 Jastreb jets over Banja Luka, the first combat engagement in NATO's history. [[1]](#source-1) In Operation Deliberate Force, from 30 August to 20 September 1995, about 400 aircraft from 15 nations struck 338 Bosnian Serb targets with 1,026 bombs, 708 of them precision-guided. [[2]](#source-2)

From 24 March to 10 June 1999, Operation Allied Force bombed the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia over Kosovo. About 1,000 aircraft flew from bases in Italy and Germany and from carriers in the Adriatic, controlled from a NATO Combined Air Operations Centre at Vicenza, and flew over 38,000 combat missions. [[3]](#source-3) Serbian SA-3 batteries shot down an F-117 Nighthawk on 27 March and, on 2 May, the F-16 of Lt. Col. David Goldfein, commander of the 555th Fighter Squadron; both pilots were rescued. [[3]](#source-3)

## Forces deployed

- **Aviano Air Base**, Italy: an Italian Air Force base that hosts the USAF's 31st Fighter Wing, the only US fighter wing south of the Alps, with the [F-16](record:f-16)s of the 510th and 555th Fighter Squadrons. [[4]](#source-4)
- **Carriers**: in 1999 the US Navy sent a task force with USS Theodore Roosevelt and an amphibious assault ship, the Royal Navy HMS Invincible with Sea Harriers, the French Navy Foch and the Italian Navy Giuseppe Garibaldi. [[3]](#source-3)
- **Albania**: a battalion of US Army [AH-64](record:ah-64d) Apaches from the 11th Aviation Regiment deployed in 1999, and a battalion of the 82nd Airborne Division's 505th Parachute Infantry Regiment secured Tirana airfield and the Apache refuelling sites. [[3]](#source-3)
- **Camp Bondsteel**, Kosovo: the headquarters of the Kosovo Force (KFOR) and its US Army-led Regional Command-East; 52 helipads were built on its west side in August 1999. [[5]](#source-5)
- **Tuzla**, Bosnia and Herzegovina: placed under the UN Protection Force in 1992, the airfield became the main hub of NATO's Implementation Force (IFOR) from 1996. [[6]](#source-6)
