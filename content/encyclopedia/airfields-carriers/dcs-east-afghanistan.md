## Coverage

East Afghanistan is one of the three regions, Southwest, East and North, in which the [DCS: Afghanistan](record:dcs-afghanistan) map is sold; the whole map can be bought instead and updates as each region is released. [[1]](#source-1) It is in early access. Its mountains are broken by farmland around Bagram and along the rivers, and Kabul is modelled with hospitals, historical sites, monuments and neighbourhoods. [[1]](#source-1) Its ten detailed airfields are Bagram, Kabul, Jalalabad, Gardez, Khost, Sharana and Bamyan, with heliports at Urgoon, Ghazni and Khost. [[1]](#source-1) Players who own only one region can still fly online across the whole map, with the regions they do not own shown in lower detail. [[1]](#source-1)
