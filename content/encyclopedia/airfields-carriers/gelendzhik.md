## Role today

Gelendzhik serves the resort town and nearby Novorossiysk; it reopened to passenger flights on 18 July 2025 after three years closed following Russia's invasion of Ukraine. [[1]](#source-1) [[2]](#source-2) The town also has Gelendzhik-Bukhta, a seaplane aerodrome on the bay belonging to the Beriev aircraft company's test base. [[2]](#source-2)

## History

Before the Second World War the Black Sea Fleet air force's 83rd Independent Reconnaissance Squadron was based around Gelendzhik. [[2]](#source-2) Just after the war the Solntsedar airfield was built on the edge of the town for the fleet's aviation; after rebuilding in 1956 it passed to civil aviation and served Aeroflot as Gelendzhik's airport, with An-24s, Yak-40s and L-410s. [[2]](#source-2) On 7 January 1982 an Aeroflot L-410M crashed near the airport, killing 18. [[2]](#source-2)

In 2004 a complete rebuilding was decided, with a new terminal and a new 3,100 m runway; the first aircraft used the runway on 25 December 2008, and the first scheduled flight, an Aeroflot A320 from Moscow, landed on 29 May 2010. [[2]](#source-2)

## The airfield

A single asphalt runway, 01/19, 3,100 m long and 45 m wide. [[1]](#source-1) [[2]](#source-2)
