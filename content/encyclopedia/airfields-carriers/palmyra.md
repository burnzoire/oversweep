## Role today

Palmyra Airport is a military airport serving the city of Palmyra, once known as Tadmur Airport and Camp Mermoz. [[1]](#source-1) The Revolutionary Commando Army took it in December 2024, and it has no scheduled flights. [[1]](#source-1)

## History

The Islamic State captured it on 20 May 2015; the Syrian army retook it by 27 March 2016, lost it again eight months later in the December 2016 offensive, and recaptured it in March 2017. [[1]](#source-1)

## The airfield

One asphalt runway, 08/26, 2,880 m long. [[1]](#source-1)
