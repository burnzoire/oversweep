## Era

The DCS map is built around present-day bases, such as Russia's Khmeimim and the US helicopter base at Harab-Ishq, but its makers say it supports missions from modern times to historical reenactments. [[1]](#source-1)

## Historical significance

The region has seen some of the largest air battles since the Second World War. In the Yom Kippur War of October 1973, Syria attacked Israeli positions on the Golan Heights while Egypt crossed the Suez Canal; Israel pushed the Syrians back and counter-attacked towards Damascus. [[3]](#source-3) On 9 June 1982, early in the Lebanon War, the Israeli Air Force's Operation Mole Cricket 19 destroyed 29 of the 30 Syrian surface-to-air missile batteries in Lebanon's Beqaa Valley and shot down 82 to 86 aircraft, the first time a Western-equipped air force destroyed a Soviet-built SAM network. [[4]](#source-4)

From 2014 a US-led coalition fought the Islamic State over Syria and Iraq in Operation Inherent Resolve; by August 2019 it had flown more than 34,000 strikes, about 80% of them by the United States. [[2]](#source-2) Russia intervened on the Syrian government's side from 2015, flying from Khmeimim, until the Assad government fell in December 2024. [[6]](#source-6)

## Forces deployed

- **[Incirlik Air Base](record:incirlik)**, Turkey: Turkish base shared with the US Air Force.
- **RAF Akrotiri**, Cyprus: a British base since the 1950s, used in the Suez Crisis in 1956, for the US strike on Libya in 1986, for strikes on the Islamic State from 2014 and for the 2018 missile strikes on Syria. [[5]](#source-5)
- **Khmeimim**, near Latakia: a Syrian air base operated by Russia under a 2015 treaty, flying Su-24, Su-25 and Su-34 strike aircraft, supplied through Russia's naval base at Tartus. [[6]](#source-6)
- **US bases in Syria** for Inherent Resolve were mostly in the north-east, including the Rmelan airbase and al-Tanf in the south-east. Some, such as Harab Isk, were closed in the 2019 withdrawal. [[2]](#source-2)
- **Carriers**: the Russian carrier Admiral Kuznetsov launched 420 sorties during the Aleppo offensive in November 2016. [[7]](#source-7) After the Hamas attack on Israel in October 2023, the US sent the Gerald R. Ford carrier strike group to the eastern Mediterranean, and later the Dwight D. Eisenhower group as well. [[8]](#source-8)
