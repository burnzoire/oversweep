## Coverage

Roughly the size of Switzerland, the map covers south-eastern England and north-eastern France, with historical airfields, towns, roads, railways and ports, Chain Home radar stations and V-1 launch sites in occupied France, as a setting for the war in Europe between 1940 and 1945. [[1]](#source-1) Its airfield list has 12 fields, eight in south-east England, among them Manston and Biggin Hill, and four in France. [[2]](#source-2)
