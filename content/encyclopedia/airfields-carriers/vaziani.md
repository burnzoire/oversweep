## Role today

Vaziani Air Base is a former Soviet airfield south-east of Tbilisi, still in limited use beside the Vaziani Military Base, the Georgian Land Forces' Eastern Command hub and home of the 4th Mechanized Brigade. [[1]](#source-1) The base hosted multinational exercises with US and NATO forces, Agile Spirit and Noble Partner among them, until Georgia suspended those ties in 2024. [[1]](#source-1)

## History

The 982nd Fighter Regiment of the Soviet Air Defence Forces moved to Vaziani in March 1946 with Yak-9s; over the next decades it flew MiG-15s, MiG-17s from 1954, MiG-21s from 1962 and MiG-23MLAs and MLDs from 1978, and in 1985 and 1986 it sent squadrons of MiG-23s to Kandahar in Afghanistan. [[2]](#source-2) After the Soviet Union broke up, the regiment was withdrawn to Tambov in Russia in December 1992 and disbanded there. [[2]](#source-2)

Russia kept the Vaziani base after Georgia became independent, as one of its four main installations in the country in the 1990s. [[1]](#source-1) Under the 1999 OSCE Istanbul agreements it undertook to leave, and the withdrawal was completed in late June 2001; in January 2002 Georgian officials reported four radiation sources left behind. [[1]](#source-1) On 9 August 2008, during the Russo-Georgian War, the Russian Air Force bombed the base; no casualties were reported and it stayed operational. [[1]](#source-1)

## The airfield

A single concrete runway, about 2,500 m long. [[3]](#source-3) The army base describes it as a Soviet-era airstrip occasionally used for support operations. [[1]](#source-1)
