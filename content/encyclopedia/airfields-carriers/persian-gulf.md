## Historical significance

During the Iran–Iraq War both sides attacked merchant shipping in the gulf and the Strait of Hormuz: from 1981 to 1988 Iraq made 283 attacks and Iran 168. [[1]](#source-1) From 24 July 1987 the US Navy escorted reflagged Kuwaiti tankers in Operation Earnest Will, the largest naval convoy operation since the Second World War, with Air Force AWACS overhead and Army special-operations helicopters of the 160th Special Operations Aviation Regiment hunting attackers at night. [[2]](#source-2)

When the frigate USS Samuel B. Roberts struck an Iranian mine on 14 April 1988, the United States answered four days later with Operation Praying Mantis, which destroyed, damaged or sank two Iranian oil platforms, two frigates, a fast-attack craft, several armed boats and two fighters. It was the largest of the US Navy's five major surface engagements since the Second World War and saw its first exchange of anti-ship missiles with opposing ships. [[3]](#source-3) On 3 July 1988 the cruiser USS Vincennes shot down Iran Air Flight 655 shortly after it left Bandar Abbas, killing all 290 people on board; the US said the crew took the Airbus A300 for an Iranian F-14. [[4]](#source-4)

## Forces deployed

- **Al Dhafra Air Base**, south of Abu Dhabi: headquarters of the UAE Air Force's Western Air Command, with F-16E/F Desert Falcons and Mirage 2000-9s. [[5]](#source-5) Since January 2002 it has hosted the US Air Force's 380th Air Expeditionary Wing, which has flown the [F-15C](record:f-15c), [F-15E](record:f-15e), F-22, KC-10, E-3 AWACS, U-2 and RQ-4; the first USAF F-35s sent to the Middle East arrived there in April 2019. France has had its own detachment there since 2008. [[5]](#source-5)
- **[Al Udeid Air Base](record:al-udeid)**, Qatar, on the gulf's southern shore.
- **Bandar Abbas**, Iran: a joint civil and military airport beside Iran's main naval port on the strait. [[4]](#source-4) [[6]](#source-6)
- **Carriers**: Praying Mantis was flown by Carrier Air Wing 11 from USS Enterprise, and the convoys of Earnest Will drew on carrier battle groups and surface action groups of the Third, Sixth and Seventh Fleets, mostly on their normal six-month deployments. [[2]](#source-2) [[3]](#source-3)
