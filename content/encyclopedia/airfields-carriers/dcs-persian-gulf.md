## Coverage

The map centres on the Strait of Hormuz, the choke point between the Persian Gulf and the rest of the world, with Iran to the north and the UAE and Oman to the south. [[1]](#source-1) It includes southern Iran with the naval port of Bandar Abbas, Qeshm Island, northern Oman and wide stretches of sea for carrier operations, and airbases in Iran, the UAE and Oman. [[1]](#source-1) Its airfield list has 29 fields, none of them in Qatar, Bahrain or Saudi Arabia. [[2]](#source-2)
