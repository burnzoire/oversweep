## From the RB 04

The RBS 15 grew out of a 1978 Saab proposal called "RB 04 Turbo": a development of the air force's [RB 04E](record:rb-04) with a turbofan engine and new wings, keeping its front end and warhead. [[1]](#source-1) Sweden chose this home-grown design over the [Harpoon](record:agm-84) in 1979. It went to sea on fast attack craft from 1985, and the Swedish Air Force received its missiles a couple of years later. [[1]](#source-1)

## In the air

The air-launched RBS 15F has been fired only from Swedish fighters: the [AJS 37 and AJ 37 Viggen](record:ajs-37) and the JAS 39 Gripen. [[1]](#source-1) It is a [subsonic](term:subsonic), fire-and-forget missile, and later versions have reached much longer ranges and gained the ability to attack land targets. [[1]](#source-1)
