## Two missiles, one shape

The Super 530 improves on the R.530. Its two versions share the same cruciform wings, tail controls and 30 kg warhead, but the 530D has a longer stainless-steel body holding a dual-thrust motor and a new seeker with Doppler filtering for low-level targets and a reprogrammable processor. [[1]](#source-1) The 530D's makers claim it can intercept up to 24,400 m, snap up 12,200 m above the launching fighter and snap down to targets at 60 m. [[1]](#source-1) The 530F arms the [Mirage F1](record:mirage-f1) and the 530D the [Mirage 2000](record:m-2000c); both are being replaced by the MICA. [[1]](#source-1)

## Combat

Iraqi Mirage F1s fired Super 530Fs during the Iran–Iraq War. In 1983 an Iraqi Mirage F1EQ shot down one of two Turkish F-100Fs that had strayed into Iraqi airspace with a 530F; the incident came to light thirty years later. [[1]](#source-1)
