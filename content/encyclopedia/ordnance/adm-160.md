## A small, cheap aircraft for the radar

The Miniature Air-Launched Decoy began as a DARPA programme in 1995 to build a small, low-cost decoy for the suppression of enemy air defences, [SEAD](term:sead). [[1]](#source-1) Its signature augmentation system, a set of active radar enhancers covering a range of frequencies, lets it look like any aircraft from a B-52 to an F-117. A turbojet flies it along a preprogrammed route of waypoints, kept on course by GPS-aided inertial navigation. [[1]](#source-1)

## Decoy and jammer

The jamming MALD-J, delivered from 2012, can work as a decoy or jam early-warning and target acquisition radars as a stand-in jammer, and has a datalink so its mission can be adjusted in flight. [[1]](#source-1)

## In combat

Its first known combat use was by Ukraine in May 2023. Ukrainian pilots describe the decoys as built to "bear the brunt of the air defenses", launched with Storm Shadow and SCALP cruise missile strikes and with [AGM-88 HARM](record:agm-88) anti-radiation missiles. [[1]](#source-1)
