## The Mauser idea

During the Second World War Mauser designed the MG 213, a revolver cannon whose rotating cylinder of chambers let it fire much faster than a conventional gun. It never saw combat, but it inspired three post-war guns: the British [ADEN](record:aden), the French [DEFA](record:defa-550), and the American M39. The Americans chose a smaller 20 mm round to raise the rate of fire and muzzle velocity, at some cost in hitting power. [[1]](#source-1)

## From Korea to the F-5

Under the GunVal programme of late 1952, M39s went into a few F-86 Sabres and into action over Korea early in 1953. The gun then became the standard armament of the F-86H, the [F-100 Super Sabre](record:f-100), the F-101 Voodoo and the [F-5](record:f-5), and the [M61 Vulcan](record:m61) later replaced it in most roles. [[1]](#source-1) Current F-5 Tiger IIs still carry the M39A2. [[1]](#source-1)
