## A smaller Avenger

The Equalizer was developed in the late 1970s from the mechanism of the 30 mm [GAU-8 Avenger](record:gau-8), scaled to fire a new NATO series of 25 mm ammunition. It is about the size of the 20 mm [M61](record:m61). [[1]](#source-1) [[2]](#source-2) An 11 kW electric motor turns it, or in external mounts a pneumatic system driven by engine bleed air. [[1]](#source-1) It normally fires 3,600 rounds per minute, at most 4,200. The AC-130 gunship limits it to 1,800, to save ammunition and barrel wear. [[1]](#source-1)

## In the Harrier

The gun was developed for the [AV-8B Harrier II](record:av-8b), which carries it in two pods on the belly: the gun in the port pod and its ammunition in the starboard one. The US, Italy and Spain all mount it in their attack jets. [[1]](#source-1) [[2]](#source-2) A lighter four-barrel version, the GAU-22/A, arms the F-35. [[1]](#source-1)
