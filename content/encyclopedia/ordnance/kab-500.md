## Laser and TV

The KAB-500L is a Soviet [laser-guided](term:lgw) bomb that entered service in 1975 and remains in service with Russia and other former Soviet states. It weighs 525 kg, 450 kg of it warhead, and Russian sources credit it with a circular error probable of 7 m. [[1]](#source-1) The KAB-500Kr, from the 1980s, is a TV-guided fire-and-forget bomb with a gimballed daylight camera under a glass dome. [[2]](#source-2) Instead of locking on to contrast, as the [AGM-65 Maverick](record:agm-65) did, it matches the scene around the target, so it can attack low-contrast targets by using the contrast of nearby terrain and objects. [[2]](#source-2)

## Satellite guidance

The KAB-500S-E is guided by GLONASS, Russia's satellite navigation system. It can be dropped from 500 to 5,000 m at 500–1,150 km/h, with a CEP of 7–12 m, and was first used in the Russian intervention in Syria in September 2015. [[1]](#source-1) The same technology is used in bigger bombs, the KAB-1500L and KAB-1500Kr. [[1]](#source-1) [[2]](#source-2)
