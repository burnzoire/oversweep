## One barrel, one gun

The GSh-30-1, also known by its GRAU index 9A-4071K, takes its name from its designers, Gryazev and Shipunov, its 30 mm calibre and its single barrel. [[1]](#source-1) It entered service in the early 1980s and is light for a 30 mm gun, at 46 kg. [[1]](#source-1) A short-recoil gun, it fires 1,500 to 1,800 rounds per minute at 900 m/s. [[1]](#source-1)

## In the Fulcrum and the Flanker

The [MiG-29](record:mig-29) carries one in the port wing root and the [Su-27](record:su-27) family one in the starboard wing root, each with 150 rounds; China's [J-11](record:j-11a) has the same installation. [[1]](#source-1) During the 2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine, a Russian Su-35 tried to shoot down a Ukrainian Navy Mi-14 with its cannon; the helicopter evaded the fire, and the fighter then destroyed it with a missile. [[1]](#source-1)
