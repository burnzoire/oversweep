## Sweden's answer to an invasion fleet

In 1949 the Swedish government asked for a radar-guided, air-launched anti-ship missile, and Saab's RB 04 was first test-launched from a Saab 29 Tunnan in early 1955. [[1]](#source-1) It is a long-range, sea-skimming, fire-and-forget missile. [[1]](#source-1) It had two modes: area attack against a big group of ships, such as an invasion fleet, and select targeting, homing on a single vessel. In area mode the early missile would only attack a ship within 1,000 m of another, and changing that distance meant a workshop hardware change. [[1]](#source-1)

## Never fired in anger

The RB 04 never saw combat. The closest it came was the "Whiskey on the Rocks" incident of 1981, when a Soviet submarine ran aground outside the Karlskrona naval base and Swedish [AJ 37 Viggens](record:ajs-37) stood on high alert with RB 04Es under their wings. [[1]](#source-1) Many of its parts, including the body and warhead, were reused in the [RBS 15](record:rbs-15). [[1]](#source-1)
