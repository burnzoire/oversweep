## The French gun

The DEFA comes from the same root as the British [ADEN](record:aden) and the American [M39](record:m39): the Mauser MG 213C, an experimental German revolver cannon that never reached production. [[1]](#source-1) The first DEFA 551 was developed in the late 1940s, and the DEFA 552 went into production in 1954. The DEFA 553 of 1971 brought a new feed, a nichrome-plated steel barrel and better electrical reliability, and the DEFA 554 of the single-seat [Mirage 2000](record:m-2000c) can fire at two rates. [[1]](#source-1) It fires the NATO standard 30 × 113 mm round. [[1]](#source-1)

## Everywhere in French service

From 1954 until the Rafale, the DEFA was the gun of every gun-armed French fighter, usually as a pair with 125 to 135 rounds each. [[1]](#source-1) It armed the Mystère, the Mirage III, the [Mirage F1](record:mirage-f1), the Super Étendard and the Jaguar, and Israeli Skyhawks, Neshers and Kfirs. [[1]](#source-1)
