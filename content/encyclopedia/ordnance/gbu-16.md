## The Navy's thousand-pounder

The GBU-16 is a 1,000 lb [Mk 83](record:mk-83) fitted with the common Paveway II laser seeker and wings. [[1]](#source-1) [[2]](#source-2) Like the rest of the family, it guides to the spot of laser energy reflected from a target that someone is lighting with a designator. [[2]](#source-2) It entered service around 1976 with the US Air Force, Navy and Marine Corps. [[1]](#source-1)

## Self-designation

The GBU-16 has mostly been a Navy weapon. In Desert Storm virtually all 219 GBU-16s were dropped by Navy A-6Es, which could lase the target themselves. [[2]](#source-2) In December 1998, during Operation Desert Fox, [F/A-18 Hornets](record:fa-18c) from USS Enterprise dropped GBU-16s on Iraq. [[2]](#source-2) Raytheon later built the GBU-48 Enhanced Paveway II, a version that adds GPS/INS guidance to the laser seeker. [[3]](#source-3)
