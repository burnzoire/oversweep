## A Gatling gun that drives itself

Most American multi-barrel aircraft guns, like the [M61](record:m61), are turned by an electric, hydraulic or pneumatic motor. The GSh-6-23 is gas-operated instead, so it powers itself, and it uses 10 pyrotechnic cocking charges, as some European revolver cannon do. [[1]](#source-1) The GSh-6-23M is credited with the highest rate of fire of any autocannon. At that rate a MiG-31 with its usual 260 rounds would empty its ammunition tank in less than two seconds. [[1]](#source-1)

## Service

The GSh-6-23 arms the MiG-31 interceptor and the Su-24 attack aircraft. [[1]](#source-1) After two Su-24s were lost to shells detonating early in 1983, and after other problems with the gun, the Soviet Air Force command stopped its use for a time; Wikipedia reports that Russian Aerospace Forces aircraft now fly with fully operational guns. [[1]](#source-1) It is also carried in the SPPU-6 gun pod, which can swivel 45° down and 45° to either side. [[1]](#source-1)
