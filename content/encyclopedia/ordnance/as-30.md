## From joystick to laser

The AS-30, a larger version of Nord Aviation's AS-20, was a precision weapon for high-value targets such as bridges and bunkers. [[1]](#source-1) It first used the AS-20's guidance, in which the pilot tracked the missile by eye and corrected its path with a small joystick while also flying the aircraft, which proved very difficult in practice. [[1]](#source-1) The AS-30L replaced that with semi-active laser homing, so the missile flies to the target without the pilot steering it. [[1]](#source-1)

## In combat

Iraqi [Mirage F1s](record:mirage-f1) used the AS-30L in the Iran–Iraq War. [[1]](#source-1) French Jaguars launched about 60 in Desert Storm and over Bosnia, with a claimed hit rate of 97 per cent. [[1]](#source-1)
