## Reading the name

Soviet bomb names are a small code. FAB marks a high-explosive aerial bomb; the number is the calibre in kilograms; and the suffix is the model year, so the FAB-250 M-62 is the 250 kg bomb of 1962. [[2]](#source-2) A second number, as in [OFAB-250-270](record:ofab-250-270), gives the real weight when it differs much from the calibre. [[2]](#source-2) High-explosive FABs are built for blast: roughly half their weight is explosive, in a case strong enough to dig into the ground or an obstacle before it bursts. [[2]](#source-2)

## Three shapes for two kinds of aircraft

The FAB-250 has gone through three main shapes. The original M-46 of 1946 and the strengthened M-54 of 1954 were blunt bombs designed to pack efficiently into the bomb bays of heavy bombers. The M-62 of 1962 was something different: a long, low-drag bomb for the external pylons of fighter-bombers. [[1]](#source-1) The difference shows in the numbers. The M-54 is 325 mm across and weighs 268 kg; the M-62 is 300 mm across, almost two metres long, and weighs 227 kg with 100 kg of explosive. [[2]](#source-2) Its American counterpart is the [Mk 82](record:mk-82). [[1]](#source-1)

The smaller FAB-100 belongs to the same family: a 100 kg bomb carrying 70 kg of explosive, 267 mm across and less than a metre long. [[2]](#source-2) Soviet practice treats general-purpose FABs as weighing 250 kg or more, and the FAB-100, FAB-250, [FAB-500](record:fab-500) and FAB-1500 became the most common sizes. [[2]](#source-2)

## Half a century of wars

The FAB-250 is unguided, has a single nose [fuze](term:fuze) and fits most Soviet aircraft, which helps explain how far it has spread. It is common across Asia and Africa, was used heavily by Soviet and Afghan forces in Afghanistan in the 1980s, and was dropped by Russian and Syrian aircraft over Syria. [[1]](#source-1) Since 2022, Russia has fitted the M-62 with UMPK planning and correction kits, wings and inertial navigation that turn it into a glide bomb; because the FAB-250 is lighter and more streamlined than the [FAB-500](record:fab-500), it glides further, a reported 80 km, so the launching aircraft can stay further from [air defences](term:air-defense). [[1]](#source-1)
