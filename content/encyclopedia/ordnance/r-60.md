## Small and agile

The R-60 was developed for the MiG-23 from the late 1960s and went into series production in 1973. At 44 kg it was one of the lightest and most agile air-to-air missiles in the world when it appeared. [[1]](#source-1) It steers with rudders at the nose; a second set of small canards ahead of them, called destabilisers, makes the rudders work better at high angles of attack. The price of the small size is a small warhead: 3 kg of tungsten expanding rod. [[1]](#source-1) Its seeker is an uncooled infrared design. [[1]](#source-1)

## Use

The R-60 has been widely exported and is still in service. [[1]](#source-1) On 20 April 1978 two were fired at Korean Air Lines Flight 902, which had strayed into Soviet airspace; one hit and tore off part of the left wing, and the airliner made an emergency landing on a frozen lake. [[1]](#source-1) In the 1998–2000 Eritrean–Ethiopian War, the R-60 and the [R-73](record:r-73) accounted for all but two of the air-to-air kills. [[2]](#source-2)
