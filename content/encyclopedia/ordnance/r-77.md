## The Russian AMRAAM

The R-77 is the Russian counterpart of the [AIM-120 AMRAAM](record:aim-120): an active-radar, fire-and-forget missile. [[1]](#source-1) Its development was badly drawn out. Work began in 1982 at the Molniya bureau in Soviet Ukraine and was unfinished when the Soviet Union broke up; for years only the RVV-AE export model was made, and the Russian Aerospace Forces took the R-77-1 into service only in 2015. [[1]](#source-1)

## Grid fins

For its first forty years the R-77 steered with grid fins, lattice-like tail surfaces. The airflow separation they cause at high angles of attack helps the missile turn, up to 150° per second, but they add drag and radar cross-section, so newer versions return to conventional fins. [[1]](#source-1) Its guidance is inertial with data-link updates on the way out and active radar at the end, and a laser proximity fuze sets off the warhead. [[1]](#source-1)
