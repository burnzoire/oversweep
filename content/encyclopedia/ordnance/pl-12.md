## China's AMRAAM

The PL-12 is considered comparable to the American [AIM-120](record:aim-120) and the Russian [R-77](record:r-77). [[1]](#source-1) Development began in 1997 with help from Russia's Vympel and Agat, test firings succeeded in 2004 and the missile entered Chinese air force service in 2005. Early batches reportedly used the R-77's radar seeker, but by 2018 the missile no longer depended on Russian parts. [[1]](#source-1) It is guided over a data link on the way out and by its own radar at the end. [[1]](#source-1)

Exported as the SD-10, it is the beyond-visual-range missile of the [JF-17](record:jf-17), which carries it alongside the [PL-5](record:pl-5). [[1]](#source-1) [[2]](#source-2) It has been succeeded by the PL-15. [[1]](#source-1)
