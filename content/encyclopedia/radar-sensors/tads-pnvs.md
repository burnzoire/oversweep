## Two sights in one unit

The TADS/PNVS is the sensor and targeting unit on the nose of the [AH-64 Apache](record:ah-64d). The two systems are independent but housed together. [[1]](#source-1)

## TADS: the gunner's sight

The Target Acquisition and Designation Sights carry stabilised electro-optical sensors, a laser rangefinder and a laser target designator. [[1]](#source-1) Inside are a thermal camera and a monochrome daylight TV camera. [[1]](#source-1) The turret can move independently of the PNVS, or follow the crew member's head, so its picture appears on the helmet sight laid over the view of the battlefield. [[1]](#source-1)

## PNVS: the pilot's night eyes

Above the TADS, the Pilot Night Vision System holds an infrared camera slaved to the pilot's head. [[1]](#source-1) It slews at up to 120 degrees per second so it can keep up with the pilot looking around. [[1]](#source-1) The Arrowhead upgrade modernised both sights from the mid-2000s. [[1]](#source-1)
