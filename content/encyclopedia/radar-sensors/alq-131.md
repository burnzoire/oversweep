## A pod built from modules

The ALQ-131 gives US Air Force aircraft, and those of foreign customers, [self-protection jamming](term:self-protection-jamming) against radar-guided threats. [[1]](#source-1) It is modular: receivers, antennas and powerful transmitters in modules, so a pod can be set up for one to five frequency bands by choosing which to fit. [[1]](#source-1) It has both noise and deception jamming, and is programmed on the ground for the threats expected. [[1]](#source-1)

## Jamming and listening

The Block II is meant for dense threat environments, against pulse-Doppler and continuous-wave radars that need high-duty-cycle jamming. [[1]](#source-1) Its receiver/processor identifies signals and manages the jamming power, and it runs "look-through", pausing periodically to watch the threats while jamming continues. [[1]](#source-1) Its flight software is updated about every two years, or as the threat changes. [[1]](#source-1)
