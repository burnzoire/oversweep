## A family of Phantom radars

Westinghouse built the APQ-120 for the [F-4E](record:f-4). [[1]](#source-1) Its line goes back to the Aero-13 radar of the early 1950s, which was designed as one cylindrical module that slid into an aircraft's nose instead of separate boxes spread around the airframe. [[1]](#source-1) A later version, the Aero-1A, added a continuous-wave illuminator so the radar could guide semi-active missiles, and later Phantom radars such as the APQ-72 also carried one. [[1]](#source-1)

## Room for the gun

The APQ-120 was fully solid-state and much more compact than the radars before it. That let it fit in the nose alongside the F-4E's cannon. [[1]](#source-1) The FAS lists it as the F-4's fire-control radar alongside the AJB-7 bombing system and the APR-36 [radar warning receiver](term:radar-warning-receiver). [[2]](#source-2)
