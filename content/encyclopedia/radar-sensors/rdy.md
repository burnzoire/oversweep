## Three radars for one fighter

The first 37 [Mirage 2000Cs](record:m-2000c) flew with the RDM radar. [[2]](#source-2) From 1987 the 2000C received the RDI (Radar Doppler à Impulsion), with a range of about 150 km and paired with the Super 530D missile. [[2]](#source-2) Like the RDM, the RDI works in air-to-air mode only. [[1]](#source-1)

## The RDY

Thomson-CSF developed the RDY (Radar Doppler Multitarget) for the Mirage 2000-5, starting in 1984. [[1]](#source-1) It added air-to-ground modes, in particular control of Exocet and Kormoran 2 anti-ship missiles. [[1]](#source-1) Its flat-plate antenna scans a 3.5-degree beam over a 60-degree cone, and it can detect 24 targets, track 8 and engage 4 at a time. [[1]](#source-1) It can lock several targets at first contact to build a shoot list for the MICA missile. [[2]](#source-2)

## RDY-2

The RDY-2 has slightly more range and adds synthetic aperture mapping; it went into Greek Mirage 2000-5 Mk 2s and the UAE's 2000-9s. [[1]](#source-1) [[2]](#source-2)
