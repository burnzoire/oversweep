## A Chinese radar for a light fighter

The KLJ-7 is an X-band fire-control radar from China's Nanjing Research Institute of Electronics Technology. [[1]](#source-1) In 2010 Pakistan announced it would be produced at the Pakistan Aeronautical Complex for the [JF-17](record:jf-17). [[1]](#source-1) Its mechanically steered planar antenna resembles Russian radars China imported in the 1990s as benchmarks for its own designs. [[1]](#source-1)

## Modes and weapons

It has air-to-air modes for beyond-visual-range and close-in fighting, ground and sea modes, and anti-jamming features. [[1]](#source-1) It supports the PL-9C and PL-12 (SD-10) missiles. [[1]](#source-1)

## Treat the ranges with care

Different sources give different detection ranges, and against targets of different sizes. [[1]](#source-1) Use them to compare, not as a measure of what the radar does in service.
