## A family of French radars

The Cyrano radars are a family of French aircraft radars built from the 1960s, first for the Mirage IIIC and the Saab Draken and then, in new versions, for later Dassault aircraft. [[2]](#source-2) The first, Cyrano I, was designed to intercept bombers at high altitude. [[2]](#source-2)

## On the Mirage F1

The French Air Force wanted the [Mirage F1](record:mirage-f1) as an all-weather interceptor, so the first production F1Cs carried the Cyrano IV, developed from the Mirage IIIE's Cyrano II. [[1]](#source-1) It acquires and tracks air targets, maps the ground and gives terrain avoidance. [[1]](#source-1) The later Cyrano IV-1 added a limited ability to look down. [[1]](#source-1)

## Upgrades

As the F1 moved into reconnaissance and ground attack, the radar gained modes: the IVM-R brought extra ground and contour mapping, and the IVM sea search and air-to-ground ranging. [[1]](#source-1) Iraq's export versions could guide Exocet attacks on ships. [[1]](#source-1)
