## The Air Force's advanced pod

In August 2001 the US Air Force chose Lockheed Martin's Sniper, designated AN/AAQ-33, to win its Advanced Targeting Pod competition, first for the F-16 and F-15E. [[1]](#source-1) It replaced [LANTIRN](record:lantirn)'s targeting pod and is lighter, with less drag than the systems it replaced. [[1]](#source-1)

## What is in it

The pod has a high-definition mid-wave infrared sensor and an HDTV camera. It also carries a dual-mode laser, a [laser spot tracker](term:lst), a laser marker, a video downlink and a recorder. [[1]](#source-1) It can generate GPS coordinates for a target, and its eye-safe laser mode is for urban combat and training. [[1]](#source-1) The downlink lets a ground controller see what the pod sees. [[1]](#source-1)

## Bombers too

As of 2025 the Air Force flies Sniper on the B-52H, B-1B, F-15E, F-16 and A-10. [[1]](#source-1) Its sensors let crews identify targets from ranges that keep them farther from enemy defences. [[1]](#source-1)
