## Self-protection for the F-16

The ALQ-184 protects the [F-16](record:f-16) and its pilot in an environment of radar-guided threats. [[1]](#source-1) Instead of spreading its power, it directs high-power jamming selectively at the emitters that matter, several at once. [[1]](#source-1) Its Rotman lens antennas are what let it aim. [[2]](#source-2)

## A pod and a decoy

The ALQ-184 was developed from the earlier ALQ-119. [[2]](#source-2) From 1996 the Air Force had some pods modified as the (V)9, which carries the ALE-50 towed radar decoy inside the pod, combining two self-protection systems that had been separate. [[1]](#source-1) Without that pod, older F-16 Block 25 and 30 aircraft cannot carry the decoy. [[1]](#source-1)
