## Night, low and precise

LANTIRN stands for Low Altitude Navigation and Targeting Infrared for Night. [[2]](#source-2) It is two pods. The navigation pod's terrain-following radar and infrared picture let a crew fly low at night, and the targeting pod finds, tracks and lases targets. [[1]](#source-1) [[2]](#source-2) Development began with Martin Marietta in September 1980, and the first production pod was delivered on 31 March 1987. [[2]](#source-2)

## The targeting pod

The AN/AAQ-14 targeting pod has a high-resolution infrared sensor, software that tracks a target on its own, and a laser designator and rangefinder for laser-guided weapons. [[2]](#source-2) Its laser takes a four-digit [laser code](term:laser-code) and can designate for the aircraft's own bombs or for another aircraft's. [[2]](#source-2) For the infrared Maverick, the pod hands the target to the missile, and the missile is launched with the pilot's consent. [[2]](#source-2)

## On the F-14

The Navy took only the targeting pod for the [F-14](record:f-14), and added GPS, an inertial unit and a laser good to 40,000 ft. [[1]](#source-1) The [Sniper](record:sniper) pod later replaced LANTIRN on Air Force fighters. [[1]](#source-1)
