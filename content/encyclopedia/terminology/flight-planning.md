    cite: [{ n: 2, page: "I-3" }, { n: 2, page: "III-26" }]

  - term: Wind correction angle
    expansion: WCA
    definition: "The angle between the course and the heading needed to hold it, found from the wind triangle. It is added to the true course if the wind is from the right and subtracted if it is from the left."
    cite: [{ n: 3, page: "16-16" }]
  - term: True heading
    expansion: TH

    cite: [{ n: 3, page: "16-16" }]
  - term: Compass heading
    definition: "The reading on the compass that is followed to remain on the desired course, found by applying the compass deviation from the aircraft's deviation card to the magnetic heading."
    cite: [{ n: 3, page: "16-16" }]
  - term: True airspeed
    expansion: TAS
    definition: "Calibrated airspeed corrected for altitude and nonstandard temperature; for a given calibrated airspeed it increases with altitude. It is the speed used for flight planning and when filing a flight plan."
    cite: [{ n: 3, page: "8-9" }]
  - term: Ground speed
    expansion: GS
    definition: "The actual speed of the aircraft over the ground: true airspeed adjusted for wind. It decreases with a headwind and increases with a tailwind."

## The words of flight planning

These are the words of planning a sortie, from the tasking to the weather and the aircraft's performance. Joint air tasking comes from the joint publication on air operations, and the Air Force's supplemental flight information manual gives the planning arithmetic. [[2]](#source-2) [[1]](#source-1)

The headings, the weather reports and the weight and balance terms are from the FAA's handbook and the US flight plan forms from its information manual; military and civil pilots share them, though other countries' weather codes and forms differ in detail. [[3]](#source-3) [[4]](#source-4) BINGO and JOKER are in the brevity words, ATO and ROE in the joint terms, NOTAM and ceiling in the airfield and ATC terms, and variation and deviation in the navigation terms.
