terms:
  - term: AEW
    expansion: Airborne early warning
    definition: The detection of enemy air or surface units by radar or other equipment carried in an airborne vehicle, and the transmitting of a warning to friendly units.
    cite: [{ n: 1, page: "5" }]
  - term: Air interdiction
    definition: "Air operations conducted to divert, disrupt, delay, or destroy the enemy's military surface capabilities before it can be brought to bear effectively against friendly forces, or to otherwise achieve objectives that are conducted at such distances from friendly forces that detailed integration of each air mission with the fire and movement of friendly forces is not required."
    note: "The distance from friendly forces, and so the lack of detailed integration, is what separates it from close air support (CAS). [1]"
    cite: [{ n: 1, page: "7" }]
  - term: Air superiority
    definition: That degree of dominance in the air battle by one force that permits the conduct of its operations at a given time and place without prohibitive interference from air and missile threats.
    cite: [{ n: 1, page: "10" }]
  - term: AOA
    expansion: Angle of attack
    definition: The angle at which relative wind meets an airfoil. It is the angle that is formed by the chord of the airfoil and the direction of the relative wind or between the chord line and the flight path.
    usage: All aviation
    cite: [{ n: 3, page: "G-3" }]
  - term: AR
    expansion: Air refueling
    definition: The refueling of an aircraft in flight by another aircraft.
    cite: [{ n: 1, page: "9" }]
  - term: ARM
    expansion: Antiradiation missile
    definition: A missile which homes passively on a radiation source.
    note: "A friendly antiradiation missile launch is called with the brevity word MAGNUM. [2]"
    cite: [{ n: 1, page: "13" }]
  - term: ATO
    expansion: Air tasking order
    definition: A method used to task and disseminate to components, subordinate units, and command and control agencies projected sorties, capabilities and/or forces to targets and specific missions.
    cite: [{ n: 1, page: "10" }]
  - term: BDA
    expansion: Battle damage assessment
    definition: The estimate of damage composed of physical and functional damage assessment, as well as target system assessment, resulting from the application of lethal or nonlethal military force.
    cite: [{ n: 1, page: "23" }]
  - term: CAP
    expansion: Combat air patrol
    definition: An aircraft patrol provided over an objective area, the force protected, the critical area of a combat zone, or in an air defense area, for the purpose of intercepting and destroying hostile aircraft before they reach their targets.
    note: "In brevity, HOT and COLD are turns in the combat air patrol toward or away from the anticipated threats. [2]"
    cite: [{ n: 1, page: "36" }]
  - term: CAS
    expansion: Close air support
    definition: Air action by fixed- and rotary-wing aircraft against hostile targets that are in close proximity to friendly forces and that require detailed integration of each air mission with the fire and movement of those forces.

    definition: In air operations, the position at which a mission leader makes radio contact with an air control agency.
    note: "The dictionary gives other meanings for land warfare and personnel recovery. [1]"
    cite: [{ n: 1, page: "46" }]
  - term: CSAR
    expansion: Combat search and rescue
    definition: The tactics, techniques, and procedures performed by forces to effect the recovery of isolated personnel during combat.

  - term: Danger close

  - term: EMCON

    cite: [{ n: 1, page: "79" }]

    expansion: Electronic warfare

    note: "The 2020 multi-service brevity publication expands EW as electromagnetic warfare. [2]"

    expansion: Forward air controller
    definition: An officer (aviator/pilot) member of the tactical air control party who, from a forward ground or airborne position, controls aircraft in close air support of ground troops.
    cite: [{ n: 1, page: "93" }]
  - term: FAC(A)
    expansion: Forward air controller (airborne)
    definition: A specifically trained and qualified aviation officer, normally an airborne extension of the tactical air control party, who exercises control from the air of aircraft engaged in close air support of ground troops.
    cite: [{ n: 1, page: "93" }]
  - term: FARP
    expansion: Forward arming and refueling point
    definition: A temporary facility, organized, equipped, and deployed to provide fuel and ammunition necessary for the employment of aviation maneuver units in combat.
    cite: [{ n: 1, page: "93" }]
  - term: Flight
    definition: "1. In Navy and Marine Corps usage, a specified group of aircraft usually engaged in a common mission. 2. The basic tactical unit in the Air Force, consisting of four or more aircraft in two or more elements. 3. A single aircraft airborne on a nonoperational mission."
    note: "The meaning differs by service: to the Air Force a flight is a unit of four or more aircraft, to the Navy and Marine Corps any group of aircraft on a common mission. [1]"
    cite: [{ n: 1, page: "88" }]

## The joint vocabulary

These are the joint terms and abbreviations a military pilot meets in briefings, tasking and doctrine. The definitions come from the Department of Defense dictionary, which sets the standard terminology for every service and for joint operations, so the Army, Marine Corps, Navy and Air Force share them. [[1]](#source-1)

A few words mean different things to different services, and the dictionary says so. A **flight** is the Air Force's basic tactical unit of four or more aircraft, but to the Navy and Marine Corps any group of aircraft on a common mission. A **wing** is a different organization in each of the Air Force, Navy and Marine Corps. Those differences are shown in each word's note. [[1]](#source-1)

The set also gives the dictionary's words for the missions aircraft fly, from strike, reconnaissance and air assault to counterair, fighter sweeps and escorts, and for the air defenses they face and the electronic attack used against them. [[1]](#source-1) Chaff, decoys and guided weapons are in the weapons terms.

Two flying terms that mean the same to civil and military pilots, angle of attack and head-up display, are defined from the FAA's handbook. [[3]](#source-3) The dictionary quoted is the 2016 amendment of JP 1-02. Later editions may word some entries differently, so check the current DOD Dictionary for a term's latest wording.
