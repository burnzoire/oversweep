## Overview

The 9K81 S-300V Antey-300, NATO reporting name **SA-12 Gladiator/Giant**, is the Soviet ground forces' top-tier air defence system. It shares the S-300 name with the [SA-10](record:sa-10) family but was built by Antey rather than Almaz, with missiles from NPO Novator, and it replaced the [SA-4](record:sa-4) (2K11 Krug). It was designed against ballistic missiles, cruise missiles and aircraft. [[1]](#source-1)

The two missiles carry different NATO names:

- **9M83, SA-12A Gladiator**: the anti-aircraft missile, maximum range around 75 km.
- **9M82, SA-12B Giant**: a larger anti-ballistic missile, out to 100 km and up to around 32 km altitude. [[1]](#source-1)

## The battalion

A typical S-300V battalion has a target-detection-and-designation unit, a guidance radar and up to six transporter-erector-launcher-and-radar vehicles (TELARs). The detection unit is the 9S457 command post, a 9S15 **Bill Board** all-round surveillance radar and a 9S19 **High Screen** sector radar; the guidance radar is the 9S32 **Grill Pan**. The 9A83 TELAR holds four 9M83 missiles and the 9A82 two 9M82s. Launcher/loader vehicles carry two missiles each, reload the TELARs and can fire under a TELAR's control. [[1]](#source-1)

Unlike the SA-10's Flap Lid, the Grill Pan can search on its own, and semi-active homing is handed to illumination radars on the TELARs. Everything rides on tracked MT-T transporters, which cross country better than the SA-10's wheeled trucks. [[1]](#source-1)

## How it engages

Both missiles use command guidance with semi-active radar homing and carry a warhead of around 150 kg. [[1]](#source-1) The Grill Pan detects a 0.1 m² target at up to 140 km and locks on at 120 km. [[1]](#source-1)

## Envelope

The 9M83 engages at 6–75 km (3.2–40 NM) and up to 25 km (82,000 ft); the 9M82 at 13–100 km and up to 30 km. The article flags both sets of figures as needing a citation, and gives no minimum altitude for either, so they are unverified. [[1]](#source-1) Figure 1 uses the 9M83, the anti-aircraft missile, and its floor is drawn dashed.

## On the radar warning receiver

The cited AN/ALR-67 threat table lists no S-300V emitter, so this page gives no RWR symbol.

## Later versions

The S-300VM (Antey-2500), NATO SA-23, has new radars and a missile with a range of up to 200 km; Venezuela bought the export version. The S-300V4 missile reaches up to 400 km. [[1]](#source-1)
