## Coverage

The map stretches from the Norwegian coast to the Kola Peninsula, with large areas of sea for carrier operations; its southern edge is near 65° N, so most of it lies inside the Arctic Circle. [[1]](#source-1) The Russian side includes the Northern Fleet's ports of Murmansk, Severomorsk and Polyarny and the air bases of Olenegorsk, Monchegorsk and Severomorsk; the Nordic side has Rovaniemi and Kemi-Tornio in Finland, Kiruna and Vidsel in Sweden and Bodø, Andøya and Lakselv in Norway, along with Swedish and Finnish road runways for dispersing aircraft. [[1]](#source-1) Its airfield list has 37 fields. [[2]](#source-2)
