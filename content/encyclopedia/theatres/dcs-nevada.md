## Coverage

The map shows the Nevada Test and Training Range as it is today: the largest contiguous air and ground space for peacetime military operations in the free world, about the size of Germany, with simulated air defence systems, mock airbases and target ranges. [[1]](#source-1) It models Nellis, Creech, Groom Lake (Area 51) and Las Vegas's international airport in detail, along with every VOR and VORTAC in the area. [[1]](#source-1) Its airfield list has 17 fields, from Nellis and the Tonopah Test Range to small strips such as Beatty, Jean and Mina. [[2]](#source-2) It suits training and present-day scenarios rather than a historical war.
