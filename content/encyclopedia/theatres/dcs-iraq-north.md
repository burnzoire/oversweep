## Coverage

Iraq North is a region of the [DCS: Iraq](record:dcs-iraq) map that can be bought on its own, and it is in early access. [[1]](#source-1) It covers the mountains, rivers and cities of northern Iraq, from Mosul to the oil fields of Kirkuk, with dams, bridges and other key infrastructure. [[1]](#source-1) It has 13 detailed airfields, among them Baghdad International, Balad Air Base, Qayyarah Air Base and Erbil International. [[1]](#source-1)
