## Coverage

The map centres on the Sinai Peninsula between the Gulf of Suez and the Gulf of Aqaba, with the Suez Canal, the Nile Delta, southern Israel and the western edges of Jordan and Saudi Arabia. [[1]](#source-1) Its makers list 20 Egyptian airfields, including Cairo International, and 9 Israeli ones, including Ben Gurion, with lighting, approach lights, PAPI, TACAN and VOR, plus military bases, seaports, air-defence emplacements and helipads. [[1]](#source-1) The game's airfield list has grown to 55 fields and reaches north beyond the peninsula to Ramat David, Damascus and Beirut. [[2]](#source-2)

The map is meant for re-enacting the Suez Crisis of 1956 and the wars of 1967 and 1973 as well as modern and fictional campaigns. [[1]](#source-1)
