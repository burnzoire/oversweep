## Coverage

The map is in early access. It covers the entire country, its deserts, marshlands, mountains and cities, with what its makers call accurate recreations of all significant airfields and military installations, and it is planned to grow into neighbouring regions. [[1]](#source-1) The Northern Iraq region, sold on its own, details the mountains, rivers, dams and cities of the north, among them Mosul and the oil fields of Kirkuk. [[2]](#source-2)
