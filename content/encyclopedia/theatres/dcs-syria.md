## Coverage

The map covers most of the eastern Mediterranean, with about 69 airfields and heliports, including the large international airports of Damascus, Beirut, Tel Aviv and Larnaca, military bases such as Incirlik, RAF Akrotiri, Russia's Khmeimim and Israel's Ramat David, and more than 100 military bases and checkpoints. [[1]](#source-1) It is built around present-day bases, such as Khmeimim and the US helicopter base at Harab-Ishq, but its makers say it supports missions from modern times to historical reenactments. [[1]](#source-1) Each airfield's radios, runways and runway beacons are in the game's terrain data. [[3]](#source-3)
