## Coverage

The Balkans theatre is one of the official Falcon BMS theatres, installed separately from the base game. [[2]](#source-2) Version 1.08.1, for BMS 4.38.1, brought new terrain, new airbases such as Batajnica, Aktion, Gjader, Klisa, Tivat and Podgorica, some updated army bases and updated campaigns. It installs into its own add-on folder and changes nothing in the main BMS installation. [[1]](#source-1) Its six dynamic campaigns can be left to the AI, though the release notes that ground troops may not reach every objective without the player's direction as commander. [[1]](#source-1)
