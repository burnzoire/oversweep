## Coverage

The 1944 map freezes the islands days before the first US landings of Operation Forager, rebuilt from wartime reconnaissance mosaics and engineering plans so that bombardments, landings and carrier strikes can be placed where they happened. [[1]](#source-1) It has no post-war structures, and its period airfields, including Aslito, Ushi Point and Orote and satellite strips, have wooden revetments, sandbagged searchlights and minimal radio aids, so navigation is by compass and coastal landmarks. [[1]](#source-1) The map is free and complements the modern Marianas map. [[1]](#source-1)
