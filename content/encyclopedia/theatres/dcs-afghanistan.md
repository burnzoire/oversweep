## Coverage

The map is in early access. It covers the whole country, with the mountains of the Hindu Kush, the deserts of the south-west and the river valleys, and is being detailed region by region: the Southwest came first, with Kandahar, Herat, Bost, Camp Bastion, Chaghcharan, Dwyer, Farah, Maymana, Qala i Naw, Shindand, Tirinkot and Zaranj, then the East, with Bagram, Kabul, Gardez, Jalalabad and Khost; the North is next. [[1]](#source-1) [[2]](#source-2) A player who buys only one region can still fly over the whole map online, with the other regions in lower detail. [[2]](#source-2)

Its makers say few war zones have seen more A-10C, AH-64D, F-16C, F/A-18C, CH-47F, Mi-24P, Mi-8MTV2 and Su-25 sorties. [[1]](#source-1) Alongside the main airfields, the game's list includes forward operating bases and heliports such as FOB Salerno, Camp Dwyer and the Ghazni and Urgoon heliports. [[3]](#source-3)
