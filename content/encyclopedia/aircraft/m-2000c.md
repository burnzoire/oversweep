## Overview

The Mirage 2000 is a French single-engine delta-wing multirole fighter. It looks like the Mirage III but is an entirely new aircraft with fly-by-wire controls. [[2]](#source-2) The **Mirage 2000C** (C for *chasseur*, fighter) was the French Air Force's interceptor; later variants include the two-seat 2000N nuclear strike, 2000D conventional attack and 2000-5 multirole versions. [[1]](#source-1) [[2]](#source-2)

## Design

The tailless delta gives a large wing area, low wing loading and a high top speed, and the fly-by-wire system lets the otherwise awkward delta manoeuvre well at low speed. [[1]](#source-1) The M53 is a single-shaft afterburning turbofan. [[1]](#source-1)

## Sensors and weapons

Early 2000Cs carried the RDM radar. The RDI pulse-Doppler radar entered service in 1987 with a range of about 150 km and was paired with the Super 530D semi-active radar missile, an improvement on the Super 530F. Magic II infrared missiles and two 30 mm DEFA cannon complete the air-to-air fit. [[1]](#source-1)

## In service

The Mirage 2000C made its combat debut in the 1991 Gulf War. French Mirage 2000s went on to fly over Bosnia, Kosovo, Afghanistan and Syria. Exported 2000s have fought with India in the Kargil War and later clashes, and with the UAE in the Gulf War and Yemen. [[1]](#source-1)
