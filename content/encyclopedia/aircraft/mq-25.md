## A tanker with no crew

The MQ-25 came out of the Navy's Carrier-Based Aerial-Refueling System programme. Its job is to take over carrier tanking from Super Hornets, which spend 20 to 30 percent of their sorties refuelling other aircraft. [[1]](#source-1) It first flew on 19 September 2019. [[1]](#source-1)

## First fuel passed

On 4 June 2021 an MQ-25 test aircraft refuelled an F/A-18F Super Hornet from its Cobham refuelling store. [[1]](#source-1) The Navy's goal is 15,000 lb of fuel to four to six aircraft 500 NM from the carrier, which would stretch a Super Hornet's combat radius from about 450 NM to more than 700 NM.
