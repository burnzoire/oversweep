## Overview

The Aermacchi MB-339 is an Italian two-seat advanced jet trainer and light attack aircraft, developed in the 1970s to replace the Italian Air Force's MB-326, from which its design was derived. [[1]](#source-1)

## Design

The MB-339 has a straight wing, a single Viper turbojet and tandem seats with the instructor behind and above the student. Later models such as the MB-339C have the more powerful Viper 680. Under the wings it can carry gun pods, rockets and bombs for weapons training or light attack. [[1]](#source-1)

## In service

The MB-339 trains Italian Air Force pilots and is flown by the *Frecce Tricolori* aerobatic team. [[1]](#source-1) It has been used in combat by Argentine Naval Aviation in the 1982 Falklands War, when six were based at Port Stanley, and by Eritrea in the 1998–2000 war with Ethiopia.
