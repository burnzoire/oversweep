## Overview

The J-11 is the Chinese-built version of the Soviet-designed Sukhoi Su-27SK. China bought Su-27SKs from Russia in the 1990s and then a licence to assemble its own at Shenyang, with engines, radars and avionics imported from Russia. [[1]](#source-1)

## Design

The **J-11A** is an improved production batch of the J-11, with new cockpit displays and the ability to fire the R-77 active-radar air-to-air missile. [[1]](#source-1) Otherwise it is an Su-27SK: a large, long-range twin-engine fighter with fly-by-wire controls. [[2]](#source-2)

## In service

China's first 24 Su-27s were delivered in late 1992 and based at Wuhu, west of Shanghai, with a second batch in 1996. [[3]](#source-3) Chinese assembly began in 1998 and J-11A production in 2000; 104 or 105 J-11s and J-11As were built before China moved to the J-11B with Chinese engines and avionics. In the mid-2010s some J-11As received missile approach warning sensors. [[1]](#source-1)
