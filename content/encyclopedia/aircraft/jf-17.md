## Overview

The JF-17 Thunder is a lightweight single-engine multirole fighter developed jointly by Pakistan and China, built in both countries and flown mainly by the Pakistan Air Force. [[1]](#source-1)

## Design

The aircraft began as the Chinese FC-1 (Fighter China 1), meant to replace Pakistan's F-7 and other older fighters. It was planned to fly in 1996 but stalled when Pakistan sought higher performance in response to India's purchase of the Su-30MKI; a 1998 agreement restarted development. [[2]](#source-2) It uses the same Russian RD-93 engine family as the MiG-29. [[1]](#source-1) [[2]](#source-2)

## Sensors and weapons

The JF-17 carries a GSh-23 cannon and a mix of Chinese air-to-air missiles, including the PL-5 and the SD-10 for beyond-visual-range engagements, plus air-to-ground and anti-ship weapons. [[1]](#source-1) Serial production of the **Block 3**, with an AESA radar and the RD-93MA engine, began in December 2020. [[1]](#source-1)

## In service

The Pakistan Air Force introduced the JF-17 in 2007, and more than 200 have been built for it and for export customers, including Azerbaijan. [[1]](#source-1)
