## A tanker built on a freighter

The Il-78 is the tanker version of the Il-76 four-engined freighter. It first flew on 26 June 1983 and entered Soviet service in June 1987, after the Soviet Union's first tankers, converted Tu-16 and M-4 bombers, were judged not good enough. [[1]](#source-1)

## Three hoses

It refuels by probe and drogue from three UPAZ-1 pods, one under each wing and one on the rear fuselage. The wing pods refuel tactical aircraft and the fuselage pod heavier ones; the Il-78M has a faster UPAZ-1M in the fuselage position. [[1]](#source-1) The Il-78M, in service from 1987, is a dedicated tanker with three permanent fuselage tanks; of its 138 tonnes of fuel, 105.7 tonnes can be passed on. It has no cargo door; its ramp is kept but does not work. [[1]](#source-1) A newer Il-78M-90A is built on the modernized Il-76MD-90A. [[1]](#source-1)

## Export tankers

India's Il-78MKI tankers carry Cobham Mk32B pods rather than the Russian pods, which can refuel both Russian-built and Western aircraft. [[1]](#source-1) Algeria and China bought Il-78s second-hand from Ukraine. [[1]](#source-1)
