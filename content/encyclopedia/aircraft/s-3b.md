## A sub hunter that became a tanker

The Viking was built to hunt submarines from US Navy carriers. It first flew on 21 January 1972 and joined the fleet in 1974; 188 were built. [[1]](#source-1) As the Cold War submarine threat faded, its job widened to sea control and surface attack, and it took on carrier tanking with a [buddy store](term:buddy-store) under the wing. [[1]](#source-1)

## The carrier's tanker

As a tanker it flew with two crew rather than four. [[1]](#source-1) In the 1991 Gulf War Vikings flew as tankers as well as attack and electronic intelligence aircraft, and in 2001 they tanked over Afghanistan. [[1]](#source-1) The Viking left fleet carrier squadrons in January 2009, its missions passing to other types including the F/A-18E/F Super Hornet, and the Navy retired the type in early 2016. [[1]](#source-1)
