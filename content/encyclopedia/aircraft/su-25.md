## Overview

The Sukhoi Su-25 *Grach* ("rook"), NATO reporting name **Frogfoot**, is a subsonic, single-seat, twin-engine close air support aircraft. [[1]](#source-1)

## Design

The Su-25 is heavily armoured and easy to service in the field: its ground equipment packs into containers that the aircraft can carry itself. [[2]](#source-2) The pilot sits in a bathtub of welded titanium armour. [[1]](#source-1) The gun is a twin-barrel 30 mm GSh-30-2 under the fuselage, and the high-mounted wing carries eight weapons pylons. [[2]](#source-2)

The **Su-25T** is an anti-tank development based on the two-seat Su-25UB airframe, with the rear cockpit replaced by fuel and avionics. [[2]](#source-2) Its main anti-tank weapon is the tube-launched, laser-guided Vikhr missile; it was not produced in large numbers. [[1]](#source-1)

## In service

The first Su-25 unit, the 200th Independent Attack Squadron, deployed to Shindand in western Afghanistan on 19 July 1981, and the type flew intensively in the Soviet war there. [[1]](#source-1) Lessons from Afghanistan shaped later versions such as the Su-25T. [[2]](#source-2)
