## Overview

The Su-33 is the carrier-based version of the Su-27, built for Russia's aircraft-carrying cruiser *Admiral Kuznetsov*. Only 24 were built. [[1]](#source-1)

## Design

Compared with the Su-27, the Su-33 has a strengthened undercarriage and airframe, larger folding wings and stabilators, canards, uprated engines, a twin nose wheel and an arrester hook. The canards shorten the take-off run and improve manoeuvrability. *Admiral Kuznetsov* has no catapults, so the Su-33 launches under its own power from a ski-jump bow. [[1]](#source-1)

## In service

The Su-33 first operated from *Admiral Kuznetsov* in 1995. In 2016, ahead of the carrier's deployment to the eastern Mediterranean, at least six were fitted with the SVP-24 targeting system for strikes in Syria. [[1]](#source-1)
