## Overview

The CASA C-101 Aviojet is a Spanish two-seat advanced jet trainer and light attack aircraft. The Spanish Air Force ordered 88 as dedicated trainers, designated **E.25 Mirlo** ("Blackbird"). [[1]](#source-1)

## Design

The design stressed simplicity and economy over high performance. The two crew sit in tandem, with the rear seat staggered upward to give the instructor a better view. Its one unusual feature is a large internal weapons bay beneath the rear cockpit, which can hold a gun or other stores; six underwing hardpoints carry more. [[1]](#source-1)

## In service

In Spain the C-101 trained generations of military pilots at the Air Force Academy and flew with the *Patrulla Águila* aerobatic team. It was also exported to Chile, Honduras and Jordan; Chile retired its last in 2022. [[1]](#source-1)
