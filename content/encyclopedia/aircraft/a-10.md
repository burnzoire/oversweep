## Overview

The A-10 Thunderbolt II, known to everyone as the **Warthog**, is a single-seat, twin-turbofan, straight-wing subsonic attack aircraft. It was built for close air support (CAS): attacking armour, vehicles and troops near friendly forces. It is the only production aircraft designed solely for CAS to serve with the US Air Force. Secondary missions include combat search and rescue and airborne forward air control, for which aircraft are designated OA-10. [[1]](#source-1)

## Design

The A-10 was designed to improve on the A-1 Skyraider and built around the 30 mm GAU-8 Avenger cannon. About 1,200 lb of titanium armour protects the pilot and key systems so the aircraft can absorb damage and keep flying. It can operate from short, rough strips near the front, and its simple design needs few maintenance facilities. [[1]](#source-1)

The straight wing and high-mounted engines trade speed for low-level manoeuvrability, loiter time and short-field performance: maximum speed is under 400 kt. [[1]](#source-1)

## Sensors and weapons

The original A-10A had no radar and few sensors; in Desert Storm its pilots used the Maverick's infrared seeker as a makeshift night sight. [[1]](#source-1) The A-10C upgrade added precision-guided weapons and targeting pods such as Litening and Sniper. [[1]](#source-1) Typical loads mix Mavericks, guided or unguided bombs, cluster munitions and rockets, with a pair of Sidewinders for self-defence. [[2]](#source-2)

## In service

The A-10 has fought in Grenada, the 1991 Gulf War, the Balkans, Afghanistan, Iraq and against the Islamic State. [[1]](#source-1)
